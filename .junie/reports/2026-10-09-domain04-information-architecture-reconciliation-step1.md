## Domain04 Current-State Summary

The directory contains **25 Markdown documents, 6,056 lines**, with no additional model/image assets in the domain. It comprises a conceptual Foundation, six established entity/identity/service families, four candidate G2 families and four review records. There is no complete standalone logical data model, formal information-flow catalogue, FHIR/profile binding catalogue, Twin information model, search-result model, governed-assurance information model or context-specific capacity model.

The Foundation establishes an Information Concept as governed business meaning; eight non-hierarchical semantic categories; identity, classification, qualification, authority, responsibility, provenance, lifecycle and temporal dimensions; reified relationships; containment/collection distinctions; assemblies/views; and definition-to-accountability guidance. It expressly separates conceptual meaning from FHIR, application objects, Java, persistence and payload structures. It also preserves capability responsibility, external source authority and concept-specific lifecycles.

The six established families cover Person/Healthcare Subject, Practitioner, Organisation, Location/Care Place, Healthcare Service and Device. They contain real semantic definitions, responsibility tables, relationship models, assertion-level governance, temporal associations and candidate assembly participation. These are meaningful architecture, not simply a list of resources. Their correctness varies by proposition; neither age nor the word “canonical” makes every claim current.

The four G2 families cover Referral, Appointment/Scheduling, Episode/Encounter and Order. They remain a review package, with bounded approved decisions G2-D01–D04 inside it. G1 is closed after K1–K13 reconciliation; G2 is open; G3/later detailed clinical families have not commenced. Local G2 A–D labels denote derivation/standing within that review and **do not correspond to this report's A–H finding classifications or confer approval**.

Reviews contain approved architectural decisions, preserved uncertainties, superseded gap descriptions and rejected alternatives. Their authority must be read proposition by proposition. In particular, current Motivation/AX-05/AX-12/AX-16/assurance decisions supersede several older review gap statements. A review record is not an alternative current upstream architecture.

## Current Information Architecture Metamodel

### Defined, implicit, conflicting and undefined vocabulary

MM §2 explicitly defines **Information Concept** as an identifiable unit of governed business meaning representing an entity, activity, event, assertion, relationship, definition, collection or assembly. Categories are multi-dimensional conceptual patterns, not Java inheritance. MM §3 supplies dimensions, with no requirement that every Concept have every dimension. This is a useful current foundation (IA01).

## Digital Twin Assessment

S-LC defines a Twin as an active management construct associated with a real-world entity, coordinating information and operational activity associated with it. Its entity-specific rule specialises AX-16. AX-16's approved interpretation does not demand synchronous/atomic “lockstep”, universal Twin routing or a Twin for every search/population activity. AX-18 prevents coordination from conferring underlying domain authority.

Domain04 does **not** establish Twin = FHIR Resource, Twin = Business Actor/Role, or Twin = persistence record. Its Person, Practitioner, Device and assembly/context models are valuable information inputs. They do not, by themselves, model the relationship between an entity, its information, the active management construct, related operational activity and the boundaries of Twin-managed coordination. Healthcare Subject Context or a Practitioner assembly cannot be promoted to Twin state through naming convenience (IA16).

A Practitioner Twin may coordinate Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Endpoint and other appropriate managed representations; it is not any one of them or their unqualified collection. That upstream example supplies a constraint, not a new resource binding or Twin Information Model here. Step 1 records the absence and recommends a later bounded clarification after state/metamodel responsibilities are settled.

## Mneme / Mnemosyne Assessment

AX-05 and S-LC establish **two concurrency domains**. Mneme owns application-facing managed-information access and distributed active-state representation, observation and coordination. Mnemosyne atomically establishes authoritative durable state/version and durable management metadata. Active generation represents successfully established active state, never an alias of authoritative version. Failed/degraded convergence cannot create a valid generation; untrusted active coordination remains so until reconciled. Loss of either layer cannot promote cache/process-local state into durable truth.

Domain04 generally preserves custody versus semantic responsibility, and does not explicitly establish that Mneme is the durable authority. However, it lacks sufficient information-level expression of these settled distinctions (IA14). Merely classifying both cache and persistence as “custody”, listing temporal versioning, or using “owned/managed” in F-NAV cannot express durable commit, active trust, observation and uncertain convergence. This is an omission against current authority, not proof that every family wrongly assigns durability.

Older DOK-R1 descriptions of the Strategy/AX-05 conflict are historical evidence of a now-reconciled upstream issue (IA24). They must not override current AX-05 or resurrect Mnemosyne as the owner of active runtime coordination. Persistence authority also does not imply originating clinical/identity authority. Workflow/Praxis execution remains different from Mneme active information coordination; no new runtime service or protocol is recommended by this assessment.

## Evidence / Assurance Assessment

REQ-FND-005 now establishes independent assurance; S-ASS and B-ASS/B-IR §4 establish its approved business derivation. Domain04's old claim that Motivation lacks this basis is superseded (IA24). Current authority does not, however, automatically approve a Dokimasia component placement or a complete information model.

The required semantic distinctions are:

```text
source information
    → contextual association as evidence for an assurance purpose
    → assessment of what that evidence demonstrates
    → assurance adjudication
    → established finding/conclusion
```

Evidence inclusion can be temporal/version-specific; the evidence-relevant source state may differ from current source state. Being evidence is contextual, not an intrinsic permanent `isEvidence` attribute. Provenance, audit, evidence, assessment, assurance conclusion, Business Outcome and operational response are distinct. Source facts retain their originating responsibility. Assurance Status concerns activity progression and cannot expose an unadjudicated conclusion. Insufficient evidence establishes neither satisfaction nor non-satisfaction and differs from execution failure/uncertainty.

GOV has useful attribution/lineage and PAT has useful accountability separation, but neither sufficiently establishes the above information meaning (IA21–23). AssuredHealthcareService includes quality/accreditation/funding examples; it must not be treated as the universal governed-assurance model or a transfer of financial governance into Harmonia. Service Assurance Modeller/Guardian do not inherently approve criteria, possess clinical authority or manage the assured subject. Detailed approval/revision/lifecycle/retention matters remain unresolved upstream and must be adjudicated rather than invented.

AX-08 selects evidence of meaningful transitions/outcomes; AX-09 keeps transient operational state ephemeral unless designated evidence; AX-14 preserves distinctions; AX-18 prevents assurance consumption from conferring operational control. Accepted ADR013/016 and ADR020 audit/lifecycle duties remain valid. The report does not interpret ephemerality as permission to discard mandated durable evidence or historical truth.

## Capacity Assessment

LOC correctly distinguishes structural care-place definition from operational occupancy/readiness. DEVICE correctly distinguishes definition from instance and temporal associations. ## Clinical Information / Clinical Work Boundary Assessment

SERVICE and approved G2 decisions explicitly preserve **information concerning an activity ≠ the activity**. Referral intent/outcome is not responsibility assumption/Transfer of Care; Appointment is not Encounter; Encounter is not HealthcareServiceDelivery; Order is not proof of delivery; an outcome association is not proof of successful clinical treatment. Episode provides a longitudinal treatment perspective without owning constituent Encounters or assuming clinical, financial or assurance authority. These are current strengths (IA01/IA06).

PAT's generic Task vocabulary/result language remains materially ambiguous against B-MM's Work Order (human doing), To Do (human review/update/approval) and Task (synthetic activity) distinctions (IA13). Harmonia may record practitioner clinical decisions and clearance facts, coordinate operational consequences and integrate external work/assignment information without determining the underlying clinical work, clinical handover, EMR worklist or professional judgement. Neither patient/practitioner information nor Twin state grants that authority.

Orders/referrals/appointments/encounters are partly developed; diagnostics, medication events, procedure documentation, clinical documents, Problems/Conditions/Care Need, care plans and clinical communication remain examples/deferred families rather than sufficient current detailed semantics (IA30). Handover receipts, external clinical clearance, service context, mobile work-source authority, care progression and discharge consequences also need bounded information meaning where material (IA29). This is not a requirement to implement an EMR or to give every Feature its own Concept.

The existing ORDER OR-IR6 dependency cannot validate authority through Practitioner Role Resolution: B-DEP explicitly withdraws that assumed edge and distinguishes Clinical/Operational Privilege (IA32). ENC/G2's undifferentiated before/upon-departure publication gap is partly superseded: B-HSO/B-PROC permit earlier preparation/publication but FEAT-HSO-29 dispatch is triggered by confirmed physical exit. Signing, finalisation, publication, authorisation and departure remain distinct; detailed applicability still remains open. No replacement privilege Service or publication event is invented.
