# Durable Information Population assessment — consolidation of Classes 1–5

Date: 2026-10-20. Status: assessment for Solution Architect adjudication. This report classifies information and its purposes. It establishes no new architecture, provenance component, retention policy, fixture selection, repository structure, migration or disposal authority.

## 1. Executive summary

**The durable requirement concerns governed information, temporal associations and attributable decisions, together with deliberately selected engineering specimens. It does not concern historical processing workspaces as preservation units. No complete historical processing run has been shown to require preservation as a complete run.**

The five completed analyses establish the following consolidated position:

- **REQUIRED:** the applicable governed architecture, including complete accepted presentation and explicit unresolved standing; identified governed historical context needed to interpret it; authorised architecture-transition facts and exact changed content under the approved three-file contract; original and continuing identity ownership, representation correspondence, source/adoption attribution and attributable authority succession; maintained framework/governance source and its ordinary Git history in their respective roles.
- **VALUABLE:** the original RAD-1.0.6 identity-gap diagnostic state; representative regression characteristics and protected whole-model comparisons; actual paired Archi observations; bounded Run-001 naming comparisons; selected Run-002 containment/contained-leaf correction information and algorithm/output associations; authentic identified failures and selected historical audit-adaptation observations. These have concrete testing or diagnostic purposes. Their value does not make every surrounding file necessary.
- **SUPERSEDED:** additional copies offered only as current architecture or ordinary source history; reproducible working projections without another identified purpose; routine report/log reproduction; and blanket-only or unselected processing material for which the analyses established no independent continuing purpose. This is an information-purpose finding, not deletion authority.
- **UNCLEAR:** the additional guarantee attached to the exact intermediate IA allocation boundary; standing of migration-era presentation rosters; the enduring scope of literal historical all-member assurance; earlier native-session preservation; and stronger correctness/compatibility claims attached to broad goldens. Particular original authority facts also remain unresolved.

Classes 1 and 3 found **no current architectural or governed-presentation information existing only in processing**. Class 2 found genuine historical continuity obligations. Classes 4 and 5 found particular useful states, observations and associations, rather than a general execution-history requirement.

The architecture-provenance model remains exactly:

```text
before.tar.gz
after.tar.gz
change-review.md
```

The inventory below is an assessment, not an additional architecture-evidence component. It neither assumes that conforming historical packages already exist nor creates an obligation to reconstruct every earlier transition. Git remains the normal history for maintained framework, governance, tests and configuration.

### 2.3 Interpretation of the inventory

An information population is a coherent body of facts, state or observations serving an identified purpose. A carrier can contain several populations, and one population can have several carriers. The inventory counts information once; cross-references express additional uses rather than additional retained copies.

| Uniqueness | Meaning in this report |
| --- | --- |
| **UNIQUE** | No equivalent recoverable information was established in another authoritative/durable source. |
| **DUPLICATED** | Equivalent information is available elsewhere; the alternative is identified. Equivalent meaning is sufficient where the purpose is semantic. Exact-byte assertions require exact bytes. |
| **DERIVABLE** | The stated information can be deterministically reconstructed from identified authoritative durable inputs and rules. This is not inferred merely because a replacement specimen could be invented. |
| **PARTIALLY UNIQUE** | Some components have established equivalents or deterministic derivations; others do not. The boundary is stated. |
| **UNCLEAR** | The analyses did not establish enough equivalence/recovery evidence to decide. |

**REQUIRED** identifies a mandatory property under existing authority. **VALUABLE** identifies a specific continuing engineering purpose without architectural necessity. **SUPERSEDED** means another population satisfies the relevant purpose and no additional continuing purpose was established. **UNCLEAR** leaves the particular obligation/value for Solution Architect adjudication. A uniqueness rating is not a preservation-value rating.

Git recovery of a tracked snapshot is explicitly recognised as a durable alternative to its working copy. Absence from history at the ordinary maintained source path does not prove absence from Git. Conversely, a recoverable snapshot does not establish attributable approval, independent correctness or historical execution merely through its bytes.
