# Domain04 — Residual Semantic-Gap Assessment

**Date:** 2026-10-10. **Baseline:** `93e36722cd1241874037481227b3ce07d5e8d224`.
**Programme position:** Architecture Completion Programme stage 3, Domain04 Information Architecture Reconciliation; assessment only.
**Standing:** execution evidence, not architectural authority, adjudication, package approval or a completion declaration.

The assessment identifies **one Category A residual question: search-returned information's acceptance into Harmonia management**. It concerns the management boundary, not search syntax, result encoding, caching, pagination or storage. The current metamodel expressly leaves that meaning unresolved and prohibits downstream representation from deciding it.

**ReportedTask is D — Deliberately Deferred / Non-Blocking.** No additional generic Task meaning is demonstrably required because its Accountability intersection remains unresolved. The current four-concept Task model and upstream assurance distinctions permit downstream derivation without inventing ReportedTask.

Other assessed residuals are B, C or D. In particular, this assessment does not reinstate the older bundled managed-state/history finding or demand exhaustive information-family modelling. Accepted replay distinctions in ADR-015 still need programme-level canonical treatment, but their operation-specific contract is not demonstrated to be required to understand the generic Domain04 work/history model. No required Domain04 meaning was identified solely in the inspected legacy material.

## 1. Method, Authority and Assessment Boundary

The [Architecture Completion Plan](../../docs/markdown/architecture-completion-plan.md), [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [docs/AGENTS.md](../../docs/AGENTS.md) and [canonical Architectural Axioms](../../docs/markdown/governance/architectural-axioms.md) govern this assessment. Each concern was tested by asking whether Domain05 must supply information meaning, rather than choose how existing meaning is realised. An expressly unresolved detail was not automatically counted as a blocker. Conversely, a currently unresolved management boundary was not converted into an implementation choice merely because it affects APIs or storage.

The user-authorised assessment boundary treats the completed Version/Active Generation, managed-history, historical-representation, observable-information, Task and general runtime-AI reconciliations as closed decisions. Repository authority corroborates their material distinctions; earlier assessment reports were used only to locate concerns. This report proposes no alternative to those decisions.

Materially relevant axioms are AX-01–04 for information purpose, standards and machinery; AX-05–06 for active/durable state and contextual authority; AX-07–09 for operational security context and meaningful evidence; AX-10–12 for failure, governed availability and information access; AX-13 for the management boundary; AX-14–16 for preserved distinctions, uncertainty and coordinated progression; and AX-17–18 for truthful derivation and absence of inferred domain authority. No new architectural approach is introduced.

**A–D in this report are the user's residual classifications.** The identically lettered categories in the G2 review describe derivation/approval standing and have different meanings. Candidate G2 propositions have not been promoted by this assessment. Upstream authoritative definitions and the specific approved G2-D01–D04 decisions supply the meanings used here.

## 4. Category A Adjudication Brief — R01

### Missing Meaning

The acceptance boundary for **information returned through supported R1.x/R2.x search/retrieval**, including federated retrieval, is not established: when does Harmonia accept responsibility to maintain that returned information and its Management Context, and what governed Information Unit boundary follows?

The metamodel's §11 explicitly reserves whether returned resources, a result set, neither or both become Information Units. This is not a question about physical atomicity: Information Units are not necessarily atomic. Nor does it question the already established acceptance definition or Unit partitions.

### Architectural Basis

- [Metamodel §5](../../docs/markdown/04-information-architecture/metamodel/information-architecture-metamodel.md#5-harmonia-managed-information) makes **explicit accepted responsibility over time** the defining condition for Harmonia-managed information. Holding, consuming or presenting information does not establish it.
- [Metamodel §11](../../docs/markdown/04-information-architecture/metamodel/information-architecture-metamodel.md#11-downstream-derivation-and-retained-questions) identifies this exact search-management decision as unresolved and forbids result encoding, projection or cache treatment from determining it. [Assemblies/Views §1](../../docs/markdown/04-information-architecture/assemblies-views/assemblies-and-views.md#1-information-assembly-vs-information-view) preserves the same absence.
- [Strategy FEAT-ISE-05/08/09](../../docs/markdown/02-strategy/capabilities/business-enabling-capabilities.md#detailed-capability--feature-catalogue-intrinsic--shared-enablement), EC-05 and [Business Intrinsic Enablement §§2.2–2.3](../../docs/markdown/03-business-architecture/behaviours/05-intrinsic-enablement.md#23-health-information-access) require federated retrieval, search and access-qualified disclosure within the agreed scope. Thus the issue is not justified by a hypothetical future search capability.
- The [Business ownership matrix](../../docs/markdown/03-business-architecture/information-responsibility/information-responsibility.md#2-canonical-business-information-ownership-matrix) establishes Query Execution Context and temporary projection responsibility. It does not equate temporary projection with acceptance to manage each returned representation over time.
- AX-13 establishes ingress/egress boundaries; AX-17 prohibits inventing the unresolved internal result-management relation. AX-13 alone does not establish the member/set-to-Unit relationship expressly left open by Domain04.

### Downstream Consequence

Domain05 can design search execution, authorised projections and access to already-managed source information from existing meaning. It cannot finalise the **management contract for returned information** without deciding whether the returned representations are managed merely as projections, are independently accepted Units, form a managed result Unit, or involve another explicitly established boundary. A Bundle, DTO, cache entry or persisted result set cannot truthfully supply that decision.

Leaving this branch deferred would permit bounded Domain05 work elsewhere, but would not establish semantic completion for the supported search-management contract. This finding asks for the minimum boundary decision only; it does not require a complete search model or imply a particular answer.

### Smallest Adjudication Question

**For supported R1.x/R2.x search/retrieval, under what conditions does Harmonia accept management responsibility for returned information, and to what Information Unit boundary does that acceptance attach?**

Mark + ChatGPT need answer only that semantic question. This assessment supplies no answer, default, universal rule or new relationship.

## 6. State, History, Contradiction and Traceability Assessment

### Managed state and history

R06–R14 deliberately separate information progression, active manifestations, operational progression, historical knowledge and contextual evidence. Authoritative durable state is established by Mnemosyne; active use/convergence is Mneme's responsibility; execution progression does not establish durable information authority. A failed attempt to manifest information is operational knowledge, not automatically an Information Unit lifecycle change. An indeterminate operation is neither confirmed failure nor success.

Historical reconstruction concerns attributable recorded information and appropriate temporal context, not omniscient reconstruction of an unobserved real world. A fixed-time assembly can preserve a historical view; this alone assigns neither statutory qualification nor evidence standing. Information-significant durable history and meaningful selected evidence remain distinct from all transient observations. No existing Generation is made indefinitely valid by the narrow failure-preservation rule.

Old language that a bounded Unit/lifecycle step “does not complete” wider managed-state reconciliation records its scope. It supplies no missing semantic dependency by itself. The earlier combined history/state/convergence finding is not reinstated.
