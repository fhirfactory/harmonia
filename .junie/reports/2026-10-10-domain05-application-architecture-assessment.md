# Domain05 — Application Architecture Discovery and Reconciliation Assessment

**Date:** 2026-10-10. **Scope:** assessment only, Harmonia R1.x/R2.x Architecture Completion Programme, Domain05 discovery before canonical authoring. **Standing:** execution report and non-authoritative assessment; no architectural decision or component/service approval.

**Recommended outcome: B — Existing Application Architecture requires material reconciliation.** Authoritative Business and Information Architecture establish a substantial application-responsibility basis. Strategy and the axioms already constrain several named responsibility centres. However, the legacy execution/envelope model, information-access descriptions and assurance allocation cannot be adopted as a truthful Domain05 model without reconciliation. Domain05 need not start from an architectural vacuum, but neither can it be assembled from the current modules.

The principal unresolved application meanings concern allocation of domain behaviour versus generic coordination, managed manifestation, work/undertaking/outcome realisation, entity-specific coordination, and independent assurance execution/control. Detailed protocols, runtime products, deployment cardinality and missing diagrams are not counted as application gaps.

No Domain05 directory was created. Domains01–04, axioms, legacy documentation and implementation remain unchanged. This report neither declares Domain05 complete nor starts its canonical structure, Domain06 or the convergence/runtime implementation programme.

## 1. Method, Authority and Assessment Boundary

The assessment began from repository-held authority, with no previous-session design assumptions. The Completion Plan's current programme position and Domain03/04 indexes establish their agreed-scope semantic baselines. Document-level historical statements about earlier incomplete/frozen tasks do not reverse the later domain completion declarations. Within each document, candidates and reserved decisions retain their explicit standing.

The derivation used here is **Motivation → Strategy → Business responsibility + Information meaning → required application responsibility → assessment of existing candidates**. An existing component, Java class, cache or FHIR resource was never the starting justification for a required responsibility. Source/code inspection supplies evidence of existing claims and mechanisms, not missing architectural meaning.

**Material axioms:** AX-01–04 constrain health-information purpose, native representations and engine independence; AX-05/11 separate application-facing active management from durable authority; AX-06–09 separate information authority, policy, evidence and transient context; AX-10/15 constrain recoverability, pressure and uncertain outcomes; AX-12 requires convenient governed developer access; AX-13 constrains management and publication boundaries; AX-14 preserves distinctions; AX-16 requires observable entity/activity coordination without synchronous or universal Twin execution; AX-17 preserves architectural uncertainty; AX-18 prevents coordination or information possession from manufacturing domain authority. Assessment and reporting preserve those constraints without choosing their downstream realisation.

The six requested classes apply to **the particular meaning being assessed**, not irrevocably to every use of a name:

| Class | Assessment meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| A | Upstream-derived application responsibility; established need, not approval of its current implementation or a final component. |
| B | Candidate application construct/boundary plausibly realising that need; requires Domain05 reconciliation/adjudication. |
| C | Implementation or technology realisation. |
| D | Integration Architecture concern; application obligations at the seam are recorded separately. |
| E | Historical, explicitly superseded, or conflicting architectural meaning. |
| F | Unresolved or insufficient authority; AX-17 applies. |

This classification is separate from Domain04 G2's own A–D review categories. In particular, a G2 category A derivation does not automatically approve its containing candidate family or make it a class A Application Component.

### Q01 — Allocation of Supplied Domain Behaviour and Platform Enforcement

**Required decision:** Establish the application control boundary between capability-owned supplied behaviour and generic framework enforcement where a domain operation is invoked, validated and progressed. In particular, determine responsibility for invoking applicable domain rules/qualifications and acting on their result relative to the governed activity and information-change boundaries. This concerns who performs and enforces the necessary behaviour, not reassignment of its Business owner or selection of a Java module.

**Upstream basis:** B01/B02 preserve capability-scoped meaning and authority; B04/I07 require entity/directory/relationship governance; B05 permits reusable composition for service-delivery needs. I05 establishes deliberately supplied Ergo logic and prohibits intrinsic platform inference. S01 Seam 5 already makes Calliope reference/design authority and runtime participants consumers of definitions; Themis policy and Mnemosyne durable authority are settled.

**Existing candidate material:** L04 Ergon validation/transformation claims, L12 Provider Registry validation/approval/persistence pipeline, L02 access composition, and S01's existing boundaries. Their different domain-rule and state-control claims are input rather than a complete general application contract.

**Why adjudication is required:** Known owners and permitted logic do not alone determine the necessary invocation/enforcement responsibility at the application seams. The Provider Registry implementation split cannot truthfully establish that contract for all required managed behaviour. This is a focused allocation question where bypass or duplicate authority could result, not a demand to map every Function/Feature or create capability-specific services. A future review may find an existing bounded contract sufficient after reconciliation.

**Downstream consequence:** Without that boundary, access/storage/gateway/worker implementations would decide which domain obligations to enforce and when, or assign originating domain authority to generic machinery. Q03 depends only on the portion necessary for activity execution; no comprehensive catalogue is a prerequisite.

### Q02 — Governed Unit, Manifestation and Managed-Result Collaboration

**Required decision:** Establish the application responsibility/collaboration contract for making a required Information Unit available for active use and for establishing/accessing a managed Search Result Set. Identify responsibility for applying the relevant Unit/context completeness/coherence obligations, recognising successful manifestation versus failed attempt, and providing the governed result's own management boundary and Version-specific membership. Determine which responsibilities are performed through the existing Mneme access boundary and which supplied activity/result behaviours collaborate with it. Do not determine cache/node affinity or representation structures.

**Upstream basis:** I01 §§6.5/9/11 and I04 search §§2–5 expressly require these meanings while reserving component realisation. AX-05/S01 already allocate active access and authoritative durable establishment. AR01/03/06/07 show the necessary application work independently of technology.

**Existing candidate material:** L01 ADR-019, L02 governed read/write/convergence contract and selected reader/writer code support point access. L03/L04 context staging and L12 synchronous search supply further candidates, with the conflicts in CR01–04. None establishes complete Unit/context or managed-result obligations.

**Why adjudication is required:** A successful cache operation or returned FHIR resource does not establish that the required governed manifestation/result boundary was realised. The upstream model intentionally does not decide whether a retrieval manifests a Generation. The necessary application success/observation and collaboration boundary cannot be inferred from Hot Rod metadata or direct persistence search. Decisions about lifetime, expiry, retention, cardinality, selection algorithms and technology are not automatically blockers; consider them only if a selected bounded application use actually requires additional meaning.

**Downstream consequence:** Otherwise cache entries would become Generations by convention, Unit context could be discarded, and a Bundle or transient query object could replace the managed Version-reference result. Existing earlier-Generation preservation and AX-05 untrusted-convergence rules must both survive; this question supplies no stale fallback rule.

### Q03 — Work, Undertaking, Governed Activity and Responsibility Transfer

**Required decision:** Establish how application behaviour distinguishes reusable work definition, particular work, undertaking/execution and governed parent-work resolution, and allocates the loading → defined logic → unloading boundary. Reconcile the necessary definition/composition/instance meanings of Praxis/Ergo/Ergon/Pragma with Ponos progression and explicit durable handoff. Determine responsibility for enforcing the already approved concurrency/participation/output policies and observing continuation/completion, while preserving the domain operation's authority. No one-to-one concept/component mapping is presumed.

**Upstream basis:** I06 establishes the four Task concepts, three orthogonal controls, parent-work resolution and explicit absence of implementation mappings. B06 and I06 §4 preserve Work Order, To Do and Synthetic Task meanings. I05 establishes governed context; AX-10/15/16, M01 and S02 require recoverable, observable, uncertainty-preserving execution. S01 already separates Ponos execution, Mneme information access, Mnemosyne commit and standards/transport responsibilities.

**Existing candidate material:** L03's four-tier model; L04 Praxis blueprint, Ergon step and Pragma envelope; L14 activity/lifecycle descriptions; selected execution classes; and L01 ADR-014/015 durable transition/replay contracts. I09 explicitly records the blueprint/actual-activity tension. G01 REC-002 requires granular destination state without approving a universal task model.

**Why adjudication is required:** Existing code/configuration conflates or leaves unlinked definition, work and execution meanings. Neither an activity method nor an ACK/checkpoint identifies which undertaking participates in outcome resolution, who enforces that policy, or when application responsibility is recoverably transferred. The information model expressly refuses those mappings. Adjudication must not settle reserved TaskOutcome identity/cardinality/lifecycle, ReportedTask or detailed qualification rules by inference; if essential to a concrete use, raise the precise upstream dependency separately.

**Downstream consequence:** Thread/queue/status models would supply accidental work semantics, all undertakings could appear to determine the parent outcome, and delivery acceptance could be confused with information commitment or completed work. Domain06 would otherwise determine application continuation and recovery authority from transport behaviour. This is an application contract question before transport design.

### Q04 — Entity-Specific Twin Coordination across Access and Execution

**Required decision:** Establish the application collaboration/control contract by which the existing Digital Twin coordination construct associates and coordinates entity-specific activity with governed entity information/state across Mneme and Ponos. Identify responsibility for necessary coordination state and for observing progression, without treating the Twin as the information it uses, a second execution engine or durable authority. Preserve the established SHOULD rule and reasoned exceptions.

**Upstream basis:** S01's Candidate-Twin test, entity-specific rule and Seams 2–3; S02 COA-05; B05 composed care enablement; I01 §8's explicit Twin/Unit distinction; and AX-16/18. Entity identity, Unit identity and resource identity are already distinct.

**Existing candidate material:** S01 gives authoritative strategic coordination; L03/L04/L14 and current active-access/execution envelopes describe mechanisms that might support part of it. The inspected implementation supplies no complete application Twin contract.

**Why adjudication is required:** “Ponos executes; Twin coordinates” establishes the strategic seam, but not enough application meaning to decide how entity coordination initiates/continues governed work and is maintained through distributed failure. Generic activity/resource processing cannot establish entity-specific control by resemblance. Any necessary Twin information detail remains an I01 deferral; raise that dependency if encountered rather than invent a Twin state entity. No exhaustive archetype list or universal routing decision is needed.

**Downstream consequence:** A per-resource cache record, permanent actor, worker, queue or deployment could become the Twin by convention, or generic execution could silently bypass its entity coordination responsibility. Clinical-work authority must not be acquired through this allocation.

### Q05 — Independent Assurance Application Execution and Control

**Required decision:** Establish application execution/control responsibility for the approved independently governed assurance activity, including how evidence assessment and determination are protected from the subject's control, and how governed status versus conclusions are made available to the approved participants. Decide the application relationship to Ponos/defined activity and evidence/access responsibilities without allocating a technology, physical separation or new operational-response owner.

**Upstream basis:** M01 REQ-FND-005; S03 assurance derivation and its unresolved component allocation; B08's approved Functions/Processes/Services/Interactions; B02 assurance information responsibilities; I09 §§2.1–2.5. Independence, non-recursion, insufficiency, recipient authority and the separation from operational remediation are established requirements.

**Existing candidate material:** I09's approved Dokimasia/Assurance Praxis concept and execution deferral; S01's explicit exclusion of independent Governed Assurance from Ponos's EC-12 contribution; L03 §6.2 and L11's speculative assurance/AI descriptions; L01 Kleio evidence contracts.

**Why adjudication is required:** Generic recoverable activity execution does not establish independence of assurance determination. Upstream authority deliberately leaves application/EC-14 execution placement open. Common machinery may be compatible, but only an explicit responsibility/control arrangement can justify it. Audit custody does not allocate evaluation to Kleio. Optional AI and human participation do not establish an independent assessor. Detailed assurance information and approval/lifecycle/retention questions remain separately deferred.

**Downstream consequence:** The assured activity could determine or suppress its own outcome, or an implementation could equate status, evidence, completed work and assurance. Conversely, technology might introduce unnecessary separate infrastructure without an application reason. No outcome value set, engine or evidence-exchange contract is approved here.

**Not adjudication questions:** changing component names; adding diagrams; exhaustive Feature mappings; splitting modules; choosing products; queue/thread/session implementation; missing deployment topology; universal Twin archetype approval; mandatory AI; or deciding every retained Domain04 detail. Those do not establish necessary missing Application meaning.
