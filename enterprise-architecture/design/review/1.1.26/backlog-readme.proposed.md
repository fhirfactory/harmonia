# Harmonia Backlog Items

`docs/backlog/` is the single authoritative location for human- and agent-authored
Backlog Items. They specify work and proposed changes; their existence supplies
neither implementation authority nor architectural acceptance.

Use the [Backlog Item template](backlog-item-template.proposed.md) and follow the
[Software Development Praxis](software-development-praxis.proposed.md) for content,
traceability, authorised assignments and solution-wide acceptance. Each item title
identifies the item; its header records **Backlog Item ID** and **Backlog Item
Revision**. Reference those values and the exact source from assignments and evidence.
Reuse links rather than copying complete specifications between artefacts.

The existing `Harmonia - Task 9 - Backlog.md` is preserved historical work material;
its structure and content do not define future Backlog Items. No separate human
or AI backlog, duplicate catalogue or automated backlog service is required.
