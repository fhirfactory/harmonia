## DR-1 — Native workbench organisation and canonical parent placement

**Issued authority.** Task dispositions 1–4 establish shared navigation semantics,
projection-specific physical representation, native outer layer containers,
separate folders for canonical navigation parents, and ordinary unwrapped leaves.
The earlier specialised Home/Composition all-element folder policy is not replayed
over the accepted Continuum tree. That original parent-only policy was applied
to the first candidate, then explicitly superseded by the Solution Architect's
[clarification][clarification]. The original Task and Report retain their wording
and original standing; canonical semantic relationships and Views are unchanged.

**Original candidate application (preserved history).** The maintained Exchange adapter anchors the native layers
and emits a numbered navigation folder with one canonical element leaf for each
parent. Its folder receives a mutable representation locator, no Canonical ID.
All 218 diagnosed parents are corrected: 204 Capabilities under their accepted
Strategy scopes, 11 Topics under Application Information Architecture/Topics, and
3 Motivation Drivers under their accepted concern paths. Child navigation remains
beside the canonical parent leaf. Ordinary leaves receive no new wrapper folders.

The actual native model has **zero canonical elements detached at native layer
roots**. [Complete parent-path correspondence][parents] records all 218 old/new
paths and identities. [Representative inspection][inspection] includes Business
Capabilities, Business Enabling Capabilities, CT1 → CT2 → CT3, Care and child Topics,
and 24/7 Availability with Stakeholder context. All 1,602 canonical element records,
including every property value, compare exactly to accepted 1.1.11 and both actual
Archi returns. Names, native types, standing and canonical navigation are unchanged.

**Current correction.** Every canonical member of an established governed
containment hierarchy now has a stable numbered directory containing its single
canonical element, including leaves. Existing area-specific architecture determines
membership and parentage: the three Strategy Capability scopes use their recorded
parent/owner ancestry, including Features; Information Architecture uses its logical
Topic decomposition. Accepted 1.1.10/1.1.11 governance and unchanged canonical
navigation supply these scopes. Existing explicit directed Home assertions also
establish model containment for 212 Business Behaviour elements. The existing
state-filtered, type-selected model resolver supplies those participants without
changing their accepted navigation homes or replacing area-specific ancestry.
This reuses applicable governed semantics; it invents no universal containment
rule and treats neither ordinary relationships nor application scopes as containment.

The actual native tree contains **910 hierarchy directories**: 30 CT1, 150 CT2,
269 CT3, 179 Features, 70 Topics, 99 Business Services, 104 Business Functions
and nine Business Processes. This adds **695 leaf directories**: one CT1,
50 CT2, 194 CT3, 179 Features, 59 Topics and all 212 explicit Home subjects.
Every hierarchy member is checked,
including all leaves; scope is not limited to the original 218 symptoms. Adding
or removing a contained child leaves the existing parent's directory and canonical
placement intact. The existing three Motivation Driver folders remain navigation
contexts for Stakeholder references, without asserted semantic containment.
Non-hierarchy canonical locations, contextual allocations and reference/history
indexes retain their prior candidate treatment. [Hierarchy correspondence][hierarchy]
records every member's original/current native path; [current inspection][inspection]
includes the issued Enterprise path, leaf CT2/CT3, Feature, Care and leaf Encounter.
The [correction Report][correction-report] records the actual scope and checks
prior to the subsequent Solution Architect visual acceptance.

**Solution Architect Disposition — Accepted.** Following visual inspection of the
actual final Archi 5.10 workbench, Mark Hunter confirms that governed contained
architectural elements use stable directory-plus-canonical-element representation
irrespective of whether they currently have children. Applicable governed
architecture/model semantics determine containment; Archi does not determine or
redefine it. Capability/Feature, Topic and the explicit Home-based containment
identified during remediation are represented accordingly. Contextual/reference
navigation remains distinct from canonical containment, native ArchiMate layer
semantics remain preserved, and canonical architecture, IDs, relationships and
Views remain unchanged. The resulting Archi organisation is accepted as suitable
for ongoing architecture development by the Solution Architect. The [closure
Task][closure-task] retains the attributable disposition and exact issued wording.

## DR-2 — Contextual navigation, native-layer exceptions and reference indexes

**Issued authority.** Task dispositions 5–8 authorise contextual/reference folders
without repeated live placements, intentional native-layer exception handling,
preservation of Business Behaviour, and unchanged authored Views.

**Actual application.** Native category markers retain all 1,285 generated
navigation-only folders. All 93 contextual occurrences preserve canonical metadata:
89 Capability contexts and 4 Stakeholder contexts. Their canonical elements retain
one live leaf placement elsewhere; reference folders cannot relocate them. Source-only
Governance references use native Other; Application System Map and unpopulated
authorised domain headings also remain visible. No semantic relationship is added.

Business Behaviour retains `Business / III Business / III.4 Business Behaviour`
with its complete populated CT1/CT2/CT3/Feature contexts and all 323 represented
behaviour paths. The 212 canonical elements with explicit Home assertions receive
their own directories at those existing paths; the remaining 111 are not assigned
containment through contextual allocation alone. Canonical Strategy references, allocations and existing Stakeholder
associations remain unchanged. All 1,209 authored Views retain their exact content
signatures and original organisation, including all 1,032 native View folders.

The four canonical ValueStreams retain native Strategy representation and accepted
Business coordinates. Actual placement is under `Strategy / III.2.2 Value Streams
— Business navigation reference; native Strategy`, rather than a foreign
`III Business` branch. Business retains its accepted Value Streams path with
four explicitly qualified reference-only folders. Folder documentation identifies
each canonical subject, Exchange identifier and native placement. No type, identity,
canonical navigation or semantic relationship changes to achieve this shape.

R Cross-Cutting References is explicitly a non-decompositional index under each
applicable native layer: the ten canonical Stakeholders under Motivation and the
214 governed historical Capability identities under Strategy. Shared R coordinates,
standing and successor/predecessor metadata are preserved. No misleading foreign
architectural ownership or repeated live canonical placement is introduced.

**Representation limits / confirmation.** Reference folders expose canonical
coordinates/IDs in documentation rather than clickable element aliases. Native
Relations remains flat with its existing 1,651 relationships; relationship folder
redesign is excluded. Empty Technology and Implementation & Migration headings
represent accepted absence, not new architecture. Native display sorting and
generated folder UUIDs establish neither semantic order nor canonical identity.
The Solution Architect has completed actual workbench visual inspection and
accepts this physical organisation, including contextual/reference separation
and preserved native layer semantics. The representation limits above are
retained; no redesign or further remediation is undertaken during closure.
