## 1. Feature Realisation

This DRP realises the Feature context required to manage the governed information/task envelope through which Harmonia activity receives inputs, progresses work and expresses outputs.

### 1.1 New or Updated Feature Details

Primary candidate Feature context lies beneath the Enterprise Capability concerned with Information Unit management and execution information coordination. The DRP proposes that a Feature for **Pragma Management** be established or refined if no adequate existing Feature exists. It is expected to relate to `DataManagement.InformationUnits.DataAccessibility` and to the Enterprise Capability/Features governing work execution and handoff.

### 1.2 Associated Requirements

- Harmonia SHALL distinguish the Pragma information/task envelope from its managed Pragmaton working information.
- Mneme SHALL own Pragma information-management semantics; execution and transport components SHALL not acquire that ownership by using or carrying Pragma-associated work.
- Pragma Input and Pragma Output SHALL remain governed and correlatable.
- Pragma progression SHALL remain distinguishable from Information Unit authoritative version progression and Petasos transport state.
- Pragma management SHALL preserve applicable security, provenance and audit lineage.

### 1.3 Associated Strategy and Motivation

Associate with the existing Data Management, execution, work-handoff, provenance, security and audit architecture; applicable Axioms and Requirements are to be traversed and named during review. Any missing Feature needed to justify Pragma as an architectural construct is to be created by the architecture activity.

## 2. Proposal

Establish **Pragma** as a Mneme-managed governed information/task
envelope through which Harmonia activity receives inputs, carries
activity-related management semantics and expresses outputs.

## 3. Design Context

These drafts capture the current Harmonia design position sufficiently
to support review across CLI sessions and temporal gaps. They
deliberately define enduring architectural identity, responsibility,
semantics and constraints rather than attempting an exhaustive
requirements catalogue. Unresolved matters remain explicit for Solution

## 4. Architectural Definition

### 3.1 Identity and ownership

A **Pragma** is a governed Mneme-managed information/task envelope
associated with a Harmonia unit of activity. It is not itself the
execution engine, workflow, transport message, persistence record or
audit record.

Mneme owns Pragma management. Ponos/Praxis/Ergo/Ergon may create,
consume, progress or act upon Pragma-associated information without
acquiring information-management ownership. Petasos may transport or
durably hand off associated work without becoming Pragma owner.

### 3.2 Input / Output

Pragma provides the envelope through which activity receives governed
inputs and expresses governed outputs. New Content generated by an Ergon
is expressed as **Pragma Output**, not merely as incidental mutation of
its working data environment.

### 3.3 Pragmaton

A Pragma may contain or associate a **Pragmaton**, the managed Data
Execution Envelope contextualised for bounded execution. Pragma and
Pragmaton are not synonyms.

### 3.4 Identity and progression

Pragma needs stable identity and sufficient
correlation/causation/progression semantics to remain governable across
activity/handoff boundaries. Pragma progression is distinct from
authoritative Information Unit version progression and Petasos transport
state.

### 3.5 Security / provenance / audit

Pragma preserves or associates sufficient security, provenance and audit
correlation for governed activity. Operational security context does not
automatically become persisted business content.

### 3.6 Invariants

Pragma management belongs to Mneme. Pragma is not synonymous with Camel
Exchange, FHIR Task, PetasosMessage or a Java class. Pragma Input/Output
remains distinguishable from Pragmaton working-state changes.

## 5. Explicitly Not Prescribed

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, this proposal does not prescribe
Java classes/interfaces, DTOs, physical schemas, products, frameworks,
protocols, deployment topology, algorithms or other internal
implementation structures. Those remain solution-design space subject to
the accepted architectural semantics and constraints.

distinctions and invariants in this proposal while retaining
implementation freedom. Implementation technology alone neither
establishes nor disproves architectural conformance.

## 10. Continuum Update

The accepted DRP is to be integrated as a connected architectural delta. The entries below are architecture decisions/proposals for review, not instructions for the Technical Lead to invent missing architecture.

### 10.1 Motivation Updates

- Associate the Feature realisation with the Requirements, Axioms, Principles, Constraints, Goals/Outcomes and other Motivation elements identified in Section 1.
- Add/refine any requirement made explicit by this DRP only after architectural review; do not delegate requirement discovery to implementation.
- Preserve existing Motivation elements unchanged where they already express the required semantics.

### 10.2 Strategy Updates

- Establish/refine the Enterprise Capability and Feature context identified in Section 1.
- Map the Enterprise Capability/Features to the applicable Business and Business-Enabling capability context.
- Associate applicable Courses of Action and other Strategy elements.
- If no defensible Feature and Enterprise Capability placement can be established, stop and reconsider the architectural justification for the DRP.

### 10.3 Business Layer — Information Architecture Updates

- Establish/refine Pragma, Pragma Input and Pragma Output information semantics where they belong in the governed information model.

### 10.4 Business Layer — Feature-Scoped Function, Service and Process Updates

- Relate Pragma management to applicable work/execution Business/Enabling behaviour only where architecturally meaningful.

### 10.5 Application Layer — Information Architecture Updates

- Add application-level Pragma information contracts only where required across component boundaries.

### 10.6 Application Layer — Feature-Scoped Enterprise Capability Realisation

- Establish/refine Mneme Pragma-management behaviour and its relationships to execution/handoff interactions.

### 10.7 Application Layer — Structural / Component Updates

- Reuse Mneme, Ponos/Praxis/Ergo and Petasos components according to accepted ownership; do not invent internal implementation structure.

### 10.8 Cross-Layer and Cross-Feature Relationships

- Connect the application realisation to the Feature(s) it realises/supports rather than leaving application constructs isolated.
- Preserve explicit relationships to applicable Requirements, Axioms, Courses of Action and information concepts.
- Establish Access, Realisation, Serving, Assignment, Association, Influence or other native/specialised relationships according to their actual semantics; do not use generic Association where a stronger governed relationship is known.
- Connect related Features where the design depends on another Feature's services or information rather than duplicating that capability locally.

### 10.9 Explicitly Unaffected / No-Change Architecture

- Implementation classes, interfaces, DTOs, algorithms, products, deployment topology and internal module decomposition remain Technical Lead solution-design space unless explicitly elevated by this DRP.
- Existing architecture referenced only as context is not modified unless this section explicitly says so.
