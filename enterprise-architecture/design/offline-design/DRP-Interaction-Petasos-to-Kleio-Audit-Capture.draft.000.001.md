# Petasos–Kleio Audit Capture through Artemis

**Design Review Proposal** · Draft.000.001 · Investigation candidate pending Solution Architect review  
**Basis** · Architecture 1.1.26 · Software 0.1.0 / G03 evidence iteration 005. [DR-12][review] · [G03 adjudications][register] · [Petasos–Mneme interaction][interaction]

## 1. Governing architecture and existing correspondence

Kleio owns authoritative Audit Information Management and Audit Capture. Clients submit semantically significant occurrences and the context required to interpret them through Kleio's contract. Kleio constructs and manages immutable authoritative FHIR AuditEvents, including the use of a new linked record for correction or qualification. AuditEvent, LogEvent, Provenance, Journey observations and Assurance serve distinct purposes. Evidence records a significant fact or decision. Broker mechanics ordinarily serve operational observation. [Kleio][kleio] · [Client contract][client-contract] · [AX-08][ax08] · [Logging, AuditEvent and Provenance design][information-governance]

Mandatory capture uses Kleio's intrinsic, governed platform authority in an established security context. Themis retains policy evaluation and the applicable PEPs enforce the decisions. The audited actor's identity remains evidence of activity. When required authoritative durable capture cannot be established, the governed operation fails closed. The existing architecture makes durable establishment through a configured authoritative audit persistence target a precondition of auditable processing. Its R1/R2 independent-target allowance and cross-target harmonisation deferral remain applicable. [AX-07][ax07] · [Fail-closed constraint][fail-closed] · [Authoritative availability][availability] · [Resilient Capture design lineage][resilient]

The historical Architecture Review named an **Audit Framework DRP** for durable audit persistence, provenance and exception evidence. The bounded repository search located that mandate and the governed Kleio Audit Capture and resilient persistence material that addresses those subjects. A separately standing Audit Framework DRP was not located. Mneme Audit Draft.002 provides candidate correspondence for Mneme's protected information operations, including access, disclosure, contextualisation and significant active-state changes. Its event taxonomy and exact mandatory cases retain pending standing. [Audit Framework review mandate][audit-framework] · [Mneme Audit design][mneme-audit]

Harmonia Logging's Log-Satellite uses a bounded JVM memory buffer and a Log-Server receipt acknowledgement without a durable commit requirement. Mandatory audit evidence requires authoritative durable capture. The Log-Satellite's allocation, receipt boundary and loss/duplicate semantics therefore provide no precedent for Kleio capture. [Harmonia Logging strategy §2][logging] · [Authoritative availability][availability]

## 2. Local Petasos integration

The Solution Architect's intended local integration is a Kleio Audit.Agent/Audit.Satellite associated with each Petasos-Engine instance. Kleio's existing Audit Capture contract/library/API already supplies the client-facing role. Reuse that logical responsibility. The reviewed Kleio, Audit Framework, Mneme Audit and Logging sources establish no governed identity or allocation for a distinct Audit.Agent/Audit.Satellite at each Petasos-Engine. Record the exact identity, responsibility allocation and trust boundary as a modelling gap. The design uses **local Kleio Audit Capture integration** as a description of the proposed binding to the existing contract. [Kleio][kleio] · [Kleio detailed contract gap][cdg43] · [G03 operative instruction][task]

Petasos contributes the auditable meaning of its own operations, for example a policy-significant forwarding decision or the durable acceptance of a named handoff obligation where policy requires it. Mneme submits its own occurrences for protected information access, disclosure and construction or other operations as applicable. Correlate these by governed operation and undertaking identity, with the minimum authorised actor, decision, attempt, time and outcome information required by the owning contract. Mneme mutations provide evidence of Mneme's actions. Petasos supplies its operational assertions directly and Kleio owns their audit interpretation and representation. The mandatory occurrence set, required correlation, sensitive-data handling and precise acceptance evidence require an owning contract. [Kleio][kleio] · [Mneme Audit design][mneme-audit] · [Petasos–Mneme interaction][interaction]

## 3. Candidate staging and transaction boundary

1. The local integration validates and submits a policy-required occurrence under the Kleio Audit Capture contract to a durable Artemis audit queue. Retain sufficient attributable context and version/temporal linkage for later FHIR AuditEvent construction under Kleio. Apply Themis controls, including submission and disclosure authority independent of Petasos forwarding permission.
2. A broker commit qualifies as a staging acknowledgement only when its prerequisites and durability are established. Where a durable onward work handoff and its mandatory audit occurrence form one governed operation, use one **local broker transaction** to send both the parcel and occurrence.
3. Kleio consumes staged occurrences asynchronously, constructs canonical FHIR AuditEvents and durably establishes them through the applicable authoritative audit persistence contract. Kleio retains ownership of representation, preservation, access, correction and retention.
4. The Kleio consumer acknowledges/removes the staged occurrence only after confirming the downstream durability and required acknowledgement. An interruption after downstream persistence and before broker acknowledgement can cause redelivery. An authorised, stable occurrence identity and downstream reconciliation must establish whether the same immutable assertion already exists before acknowledging it. Preserve and adjudicate identity/content conflicts. An indeterminate downstream write leaves the occurrence recoverable for reconciliation.

**Transaction scope.** Artemis 2.33.0 supports a local transaction over both sends when the producers use the same transacted JMS session, or equivalent transactional Core session, on the same logical broker and transaction resource. The local Kleio binding must support the shared transaction under its own governed authority. Both messages must be persistent and actually route to the intended durable queue bindings before commit can support a dual-enqueue claim. A broker acknowledgement of commit supplies the staging/handoff fact only under the verified routing, journal and HA contract. Two sessions with independent commits, distinct broker resources, or a bridged/routed second hop do not share this local commit. A multi-resource design would require separately governed coordination and recovery. The broker transaction does **not** commit Mneme/Mnemosyne undertaking state, Ergon execution, or downstream AuditEvent persistence. Establish the durable undertaking and its resolvable lineage before the parcel handoff, and reconcile a work record whose handoff remains outstanding after broker failure. [Artemis messaging and transactions][messaging] · [Artemis same-server transaction scope][bridge] · [Artemis commit guarantees][guarantees] · [Artemis routing][address-model] · [ADR-018][adr]

The shared session is an adapter-level prerequisite. Preserve the broker-neutral Petasos API and establish how the Kleio binding joins the local transaction without delegating Kleio capture authority to Petasos. The candidate makes no allocation of a new deployable component. [Petasos API and governance boundaries][agents]

**Authority condition.** Recognising Kleio-governed durable staging as authoritative capture is a proposed refinement. Under Architecture 1.1.26, staging and its ACK provide transport evidence. The existing authoritative capture and availability conditions govern whether the operation may complete. Any future acceptance of staging would have to establish which evidence, authority, durability and acknowledgement satisfy those conditions at the new boundary, including whether and for how long an eligible final audit target may be unavailable. A staged occurrence and its subsequent immutable FHIR AuditEvent are distinct states of the audit lifecycle. [AX-07][ax07] · [Kleio availability][availability]

### Operation outcomes

| Outcome | Evidence and recovery consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Attempted | Petasos tried to enqueue the parcel. Preparing or sending an occurrence in the same uncommitted transaction evidences an attempt only. A handoff-committed occurrence becomes visible only on commit of the pair and must be correlated with the owning commit evidence. |
| Committed | A successful commit and the established routing and durability prerequisites support two durable enqueues. They establish neither Ergon delivery/execution nor authoritative AuditEvent persistence. Future treatment as authoritative capture requires the disposition described above. |
| Failed | A confirmed rejection or rollback establishes failure of that broker transaction. Any policy-mandated audit of a failed or denied attempt needs a separately governed durable submission. An occurrence in the rolled-back transaction cannot supply that evidence. When mandatory capture is unavailable, fail closed and preserve the failure state. |
| Indeterminate | A lost/ambiguous commit reply, failover at the commit boundary or inconclusive reconciliation leaves the original outcome indeterminate. Withhold dependent success and reconcile the same obligation and occurrence against owning Petasos and Kleio evidence before retrying or acknowledging. A confirmed rollback of a retry or replacement session supplies no result for a separate uncertain attempt. |

Per-match transactions would make pairwise handoff atomic, while fan-out retains independent results for each subscription. A policy-significant outcome outside a parcel enqueue, including denial or a failed attempt when required, follows the applicable Kleio capture contract independently. [AX-15][ax15] · [Petasos–Mneme interaction][interaction]

## 4. Artemis 2.33.0 capabilities and Harmonia implications

The repository pins Artemis 2.33.0. The findings below are based on Apache's versioned manual. Effective deployment settings, runtime recovery and equivalence of the two durability guarantees still require evidence. [Repository version][pom]

| Area | Verified capability and candidate implication |
| --- | --- |
| Durable queues and journal | Persistent messages routed to durable queues can be stored by a persistence-enabled broker. The file journal offers NIO, ASYNCIO and MAPPED modes. `journal-datasync` affects protection against power loss, particularly with NIO and MAPPED journals. Queue durability, message persistence, actual bindings, synchronisation and the storage failure model all matter. Validate both routes and their policy, since Artemis can discard unrouted traffic, including on addresses with all queues disabled. Account for paging/storage availability and prohibit capture policies that silently drop required evidence. [Persistence][persistence] · [Address model][address-model] · [Address settings][address-settings] |
| Transactional sends and commitment | Completion waits for the broker reply. With the file journal's `journal-sync-transactional=true`, Artemis synchronises the journal at transaction boundaries before replying. The documented default is true. Disabling sync weakens the assurance after an ACK. A non-transactional blocking durable send and `journal-sync-non-transactional` can provide a different, single-send guarantee when the persistent message actually routes to a durable queue. They do not couple two sends. [Send and commit guarantees][guarantees] |
| Asynchronous acknowledgements | The Core client's `SendAcknowledgementHandler` and positive `confirmationWindowSize` permit nonblocking sends and asynchronous broker confirmation. This is a send confirmation path. Bind any durability inference to the chosen routing and journal guarantees. A send callback, consumer receipt and consumer acknowledgement have distinct meanings from the local pair commit and from verified downstream AuditEvent persistence. Kleio may process asynchronously after the accepted capture point even when the enqueue transaction itself waits for a commit reply. [Send and commit guarantees][guarantees] · [Client failover][client-failover] |
| Duplicate detection and replay | Artemis can suppress sends using `_AMQ_DUPL_ID`. Its per-address circular cache has a finite size and optional persistence, and duplicate detection can cause a whole transaction to be ignored. Preserve stable identities for a permitted retry of the same pair and bind the replay scope to the original transaction. Distinct occurrences or subscriptions need distinct identities. Cache expiry/eviction, restart and topology affect the guarantee. Broker suppression alone cannot prove that the earlier pair was committed or make downstream AuditEvent construction or Ergon processing exactly-once. [Duplicate detection][duplicates] |
| Failover and recovery | Apache documents shared-store HA and primary/backup replication, with recovery confined to persisted state and client failover depending on its configuration. A failover can unblock a pending call or roll back the affected client transaction. Retain an indeterminate original commit where the client lacks conclusive evidence. Verify the actual storage, replication readiness, failover, recovery and restart guarantees for both queues, including unavailability of the required replica or storage. [HA and failover][ha] · [Client failover][client-failover] · [Duplicate detection][duplicates] |
| Redelivery and evidence retention | Rollback and recovery can cause redelivery. Dead-letter rules can move evidence out of the source queue, and a delivery limit without an appropriate dead-letter target can remove it. Any recovery/quarantine transfer requires Kleio governance and equivalent durable custody with attribution and recoverability. Expiry, purge, overflow or ordinary DLQ cleanup cannot be allowed to discard mandatory unmaterialised evidence. Queue deletion, administration, backup and recovery require corresponding evidence controls. [Redelivery][redelivery] · [Address settings][address-settings] · [Management][management] |
| Notifications and plugins | Artemis provides JMX or notification-address events and broker plugin callbacks for internal, send, delivery and other events. Use them where useful for diagnostics, control and reconciliation support. Their documented event hooks provide no independent assurance that a governed semantic occurrence was authorised, durably committed with its parcel or established as an AuditEvent. [Management notifications][management] · [Broker plugin support][plugins] |

**Durability parity.** Where Artemis stores both classes of work, the assurance for mandatory audit evidence SHALL be no weaker than the applicable durable Petasos work guarantee. Compare the actual protection across transaction acknowledgement and sync, persistent message and binding policy, paging and storage, restart, failover and replication failure domains, redelivery and recovery, and full-store, routing and other failure handling. For example, a work guarantee that requires survival of a stated replica or site loss requires at least that protection for unmaterialised audit evidence. Sharing a broker or commit alone supplies no evidence that all lifecycle policies are equivalent. The audit queue retains occurrences under Kleio's evidence custody until authorised transfer to the durable audit record. Petasos work retains its own delivery and retention lifecycle.

## 5. Dispositions and verification needed before dependent use

- Decide the governed correspondence of the local integration to the existing Kleio contract, including its identity and allocation, Themis PEP/PDP enforcement, authority delegation and transaction participation across the Petasos/Kleio boundary.
- Decide whether validated durable staging can count as authoritative capture and for which operations. If approved, reconcile the Kleio Audit Capture and availability contracts and affected constraints with AX-07 and AX-05/06. Specify admissible staged evidence and custody, sufficient commit and routing proof, independent-target correspondence, audit backlog and downstream-outage readiness limits, and fail-closed behaviour. Preserve immutable FHIR AuditEvent and longitudinal evidence requirements.
- Define the mandatory Petasos occurrence types, who may attest each outcome, the identity/replay and correlation contract, ambiguous commit reconciliation, and the separately governed path for policy-required failed or denied occurrences. Define how Kleio establishes that a replay is the same assertion and when it may acknowledge staging.
- Establish equivalent durability and prove it against the actual candidate configuration. Verify both sends under normal commit, confirmed rollback, absent or disabled routes, full storage, broker or client crash before/after commit, lost ACK, replica loss and failover. Verify downstream outage, crash between AuditEvent persistence and staging ACK, poison occurrence custody, duplicate IDs inside/outside the broker cache window, retry of one match in partial fan-out, and access control. Architecture 1.1.26 remains the basis for these review questions. [G03 adjudications][register] · [DR-12][review]
