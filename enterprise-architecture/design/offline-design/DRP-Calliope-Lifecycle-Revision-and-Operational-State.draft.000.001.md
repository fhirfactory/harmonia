## 1. A13 — Governed information lifecycle and data state

**Accepted authority.** IU-10 and OM-10 place preservation, immutable history, retention, archive, hold and retirement with governed information. The accepted IU architecture permits concept-specific lifecycles and preserves native business state in Content. Mnemosyne establishes authoritative durable state and the management metadata needed to interpret it. Governed archive access and faithful archived versions are established obligations. [IU Final.001 §17][iu-retention] · [IU Final.002 §4.3][iu-lifecycle] · [IU Metadata][metadata] · [Business Information Unit][iu-business] · [Governed Archive Access][archive-access] · [Archived Version Fidelity][archive-fidelity]

**Adjudication.** All persistently managed information has governed information state. The applicable PersistenceService can support it natively. Where it lacks the required capability, Mnemosyne maintains a durable and coherent state overlay. Both mechanisms must produce the same governed access and mutation semantics. The overlay follows authoritative state through reads, writes and recovery, including the durability criteria applicable to the operation. Information state and native business state each retain their meaning independently of a request's processing stage or Outcome. [Issued A13][task]

| State or obligation | Governed semantics |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Carry forward the existing rules for applicable reads, discovery and changes, including Themis, exact-revision and concurrency constraints. The adjudication supplies no default-state migration or new transition. Active information is distinct from a Mneme cache manifestation. |
| Deleted | Unavailable to general Harmonia services. Ordinary searches and query reports exclude it. Applicable retention still governs the underlying information. |
| Archived | Available through authorised GET and searches that explicitly request archived information. UPDATE and DELETE are prohibited. |
| Retired | Available exclusively for logging, audit-event and provenance purposes, subject to the established access and evidence controls. |
| LegalHold | Administration is an SEP responsibility outside Harmonia's native lifecycle management. Hold obligations may apply alongside an information lifecycle state. The representation and binding to the external authority are open. |

**Reconciliation and Continuum amendments.** The state requirement makes IU Final.002's “as applicable” qualification more specific for persistently managed information. Scope it to governed persistence, preserving concept-specific lifecycles and existing obligations for current, superseded and retained information. The accepted Legal Hold Recognition and Retention-Hold Respect Features still require recognition, evidence and prevention of actions that would violate an applicable hold. SEP allocation is compatible with those obligations if Harmonia can recognise and respect the externally established restrictions. Any reading that removes those internal safeguards would conflict with accepted authority and requires explicit disposition. Refine the Business IU, IU Metadata and PersistenceService definitions and the affected GET/search/mutation contracts. Trace the external legal-hold boundary to the existing hold and retention Features. [IU Final.002 §§1, 4.3][iu-final] · [Legal Hold Recognition][hold-recognition] · [Retention Hold Protection][hold-protection] · [Retention-Hold Respect][hold-respect] · [PersistenceService][persistence] · [GovernedReader][reader] · [GovernedSummaryReader][summary-reader] · [GovernedSummarySearch][summary-search]

**Detailed design.** Specify which governed subject or revision a state applies to, the approved state and vocabulary bindings, and how existing records obtain a verified state. Specify provider capability checks, native-state mappings and the overlay's durable authority, consistency and recovery obligations. Specify how state reaches full and Summary reads, search indexes and continuations, query reports and active/cache projections, including uncertain, stale or contradictory observations. Establish the source and effective scope of external hold evidence and how lifecycle decisions respect it when that evidence is incomplete. Keep transition authority with the applicable governed contracts. [IU Metadata][metadata] · [Accepted G02][g02] · [Mneme Cache Architecture §4.6][cache-contracts]

**Implementation and verification.** Once authorised, demonstrate equivalent behaviour with native state and an overlay. Exercise authorised and denied archive GET and explicit archive search, ordinary search exclusion, prohibited archive mutations and retired evidence-only access. Verify preservation of applicable holds and retention, including on cached or indexed access, and prove coherent state after partial writes, stale projections, restart and failover. [GovernedSummarySearch][summary-search] · [Retention-Hold Respect][hold-respect] · [Mneme Cache Architecture][cache]

## 2. A14 — Logical DELETE and distinct RETIRE

**Accepted authority.** IU-10 and the accepted five-contract interaction preserve history and retention on RETIRE. The current full `GovernedWriter` definition names CREATE, UPDATE and RETIRE. The older CRUD wording that mentions DELETE is predecessor material in that element. Original interaction MI-07 preserves RETIRE and its provider-disposition mapping question. The initial `GovernedSummaryWriter` contract is limited to CREATE and RETIRE and leaves external Content under its existing custody. The earlier persistence DRP §5.1 describes CREATE/UPDATE/DELETE, with the native/disposition distinction in §8. [IU Final.001 §17][iu-retention] · [GovernedWriter][writer] · [GovernedSummaryWriter][summary-writer] · [Original MI-07][interaction-residual] · [Accepted persistence DRP §§5–8][persistence-drp] · [HCRL.004 DR-8][hcrl-delete]

**Adjudication.** A governed DELETE sets the applicable content state to **Deleted**. General Harmonia services cannot access the deleted information, and ordinary searches and query reports exclude it. The underlying information remains subject to applicable retention. External, offline physical clean-up and destruction are governed by applicable data-retention legislation and policy. RETIRE continues to apply IU retirement and preserve historical identity, required versions and evidence. Retired access has the purpose limits in A13. Archived preserves authorised retrieval and explicitly requested search access while prohibiting UPDATE and DELETE. [Issued A14][task]

**Reconciliation and Continuum amendments.** The adjudicated DELETE has its own result and cannot be bound as an alias for RETIRE. There is a genuine contract delta wherever the current RETIRE-based full-write wording would be used to bind DELETE. The older CRUD wording supplies no current binding for setting Deleted. Record the new operation in the IU lifecycle and affected persistence/access contracts through ADD/HCRL. Confirm where it belongs before amending the operation inventory. Reconcile MI-07, provider capability and adapter mappings, and search/report visibility. Preserve the five-contract boundary and Summary CREATE/RETIRE scope unless separately adjudicated. NFR-118's policy support and the enterprise's Scheduled Record Disposition obligation can coexist with external offline disposal. Qualify any provider-disposition wording that could allocate physical clean-up to Harmonia. [GovernedWriter][writer] · [GovernedSummaryWriter][summary-writer] · [PersistenceService][persistence] · [Configurable Retention and Disposal][retention-policy] · [Scheduled Record Disposition][scheduled-disposition] · [Architecture Review Praxis][review-praxis]

The Business IU incorporates a categorical rule that Information Concepts “once asserted or recorded into durable truth, are never subjected to physical destructive deletion.” Logical DELETE complies with that rule. There is a scope conflict if external offline disposal is intended to include information that the categorical rule protects. Preserve the current prohibition pending explicit Solution Architect treatment of that overlap. Capture the decided boundary in the Business IU and applicable retention references through governed incorporation. [Business IU, source-qualified lifecycle §3][iu-history]

**Detailed design.** Establish the authorised DELETE path and what content or revision it targets. Reconcile the access restriction with exact-revision and as-of reconstruction obligations and with the full and Summary projections for the same IU. Confirm the scope for a Summary that registers externally held Content before mapping any DELETE request there. Specify per-provider logical state mappings, retention/hold checks, transition evidence and safe handling of retry and uncertain completion. [Business IU, source-qualified lifecycle §3][iu-history] · [GovernedSummaryWriter][summary-writer] · [GovernedReader][reader] · [Persistence Adapter][adapter] · [HCRL.004 DR-8][hcrl-delete]

**Implementation and verification.** Once authorised, prove DELETE changes state without destroying retained content, history or evidence and that no provider path invokes physical deletion to achieve it. Verify that point reads, ordinary searches, query reports and cache/index refresh enforce visibility, and that RETIRE and DELETE produce their respective effects. Exercise archival or hold rejection, retries, and recovery after an uncertain response. Confirm that Summary retirement leaves the external referent untouched. [GovernedWriter][writer] · [GovernedSummaryWriter][summary-writer] · [Mnemosyne persistence DRP][persistence-drp]

## 3. A15 — Data Version and Managed Revision

**Accepted authority.** The accepted IU Class uses `InformationUnit.version` for the Mnemosyne-established authoritative persisted IU revision. AV-01/04 use “version” for exact authoritative reads and the actual revision returned by a successful write, including a higher revision where required to preserve immutable history. Mneme owns individual-IU active coordination and operational revision. Active Generation identifies a cache manifestation. Content Structure Version and optional native Content version have separate meanings. [IU Final.002 §2.2][iu-revisions] · [IU Class][iu-class] · [RAD 1.1.25 DR-4][rad25-revisions] · [GovernedReader][reader] · [GovernedWriter][writer] · [Mneme Cache Architecture][cache]

**Adjudication.** **Data Version** identifies the data. The applicable PersistenceService determines the principal Data Version under its documented contract. It may use a native `version` attribute, a native `revision` attribute, or select or compose a value from supplied attributes. The underlying information may supply both attributes, either or neither. Preserve the supplied attributes and their native meanings. A Data Version may be absent, nonnumeric, unordered or nonsequential. **Managed Revision** supports governed management, concurrency, availability and coordination between active and authoritative representations. It is established independently of Data Version. Expected-revision checks use the revision defined by the management contract. [Issued A15][task]

| Concern | Responsibility and contract meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Authoritative Managed Revision | Mnemosyne establishes and returns the authoritative persisted IU revision. It belongs with the IU and is read only under the accepted durability rules. |
| Mneme operational revision | Mneme owns active concurrency and coherence. The operational revision is associated peer OperationalMetadata with the applicable existing projections. |
| Active Generation | Mneme identifies an established active manifestation. Multiple generations can express one operational revision. |
| Native attributes and Data Version | Native attributes retain their original values and meaning. The PersistenceService owns the documented principal Data Version mapping and its absence semantics. |
| Content Structure Version and TopicEntry version | Retain their own structure and Topic semantics. TopicEntry version treatment stays with the separate Topic review and G02 correspondence. |

**Reconciliation and Continuum amendments.** The word “version” in `InformationUnit.version`, `GovernedReader(version=*)`, existing IU version references and Writer results currently denotes the authoritative Managed Revision. Reconcile that terminology in the canonical IU Class, Metadata, PersistenceService, Reader/Writer and Summary/Search references where they expose these meanings. Preserve exact revision selection, the actual revision returned on Success, and the existing active and historical reference meanings. Explicitly allocate principal Data Version selection to PersistenceService. Physical field names and wire bindings remain questions for detailed design and review. [IU Class][iu-class] · [IU Metadata][metadata] · [GovernedWriter][writer] · [GovernedSummarySearch][summary-search] · [PersistenceService][persistence] · [Accepted G02][g02] · [Topic/TopicMask Draft.001.003][topic-draft]

**Detailed design.** Specify each PersistenceService's supported native inputs and deterministic Version selection or composition, representation, fidelity and absence rules. Specify the authoritative revision's scope, representation, progression and comparison rules, including expected-revision checks, missing, stale or unsupported comparisons and their governed result. Specify how Mneme compares operational revisions, relates a manifestation or generation to an authoritative revision and reconciles them after a write or read-back. Preserve applicable historical bindings and distinguish revision criteria from native attributes and Content Structure or TopicEntry versions. Any unavoidable change to an accepted binding requires explicit review. [GovernedWriter][writer] · [IU Final.002 §2.2][iu-revisions] · [RAD 1.1.24 DR-1][rad24] · [G02 reconciliation][g02-evidence]

**Implementation and verification.** Once authorised, demonstrate fidelity for both native attributes, either alone, and neither, including opaque values and provider-specific compositions. Exercise exact-revision reads, expected-revision matches and stale or unsupported comparisons without lossy numeric defaults, concurrent writes, preserved immutable history and reconciliation of active generations against the actual authoritative revision. Demonstrate that changes to one version domain have only their governed effects. [GovernedReader][reader] · [GovernedWriter][writer] · [Mneme Cache Architecture][cache] · [Existing review evidence][groups]

## 4. A16 — Synchronous transactions, Outcomes and operational ownership

**Accepted authority.** All five accepted contracts are synchronous governed request/response. AV-03/04 require verified multi-site authoritative durability before reporting Success for authoritative writes. The same requirement applies to authoritative Summary mutations. MP-04/06 retain the Calliope-owned factual Outcome and preserve its scope, supporting provider verification and Mneme-level consumer disposition. OM-06–OM-08 define peer OperationalMetadata and preserve the accepted façade projections, including the operational facts and evidence required by their contracts. [RAD 1.1.24 DR-1][rad24] · [RAD 1.1.23 DR-1][rad23] · [Continuum Outcome][outcome] · [RAD 1.1.25 DR-2][rad25-operational] · [OperationalMetadata DRP][om]

**Adjudication.** All transactions across Mneme/Mnemosyne are synchronous. The response reports the result against the applicable operation's success criteria. **Success** certifies those criteria, including required multi-site durability. **Failure** reports an established failure against the declared criteria. **Indeterminate** preserves the inability to establish the result at reporting time. Receipt, native acknowledgement, work acceptance, internal stage changes, local commit or queued replication alone cannot establish a required multi-site effect. A transport timeout or lost response leaves the factual transaction outcome open. Mneme may determine failure for its own operation while preserving Mnemosyne's reported or unknown persistence outcome. [Issued A16][task] · [Interaction DRP §§4.8–4.9][interaction-write] · [Continuum Outcome][outcome]

Processing status, supervision and recovery belong inside the responsible component, with existing controlled observability and operational management. The shared IU requires no general transaction-progress structure, universal observation catalogue or `Recovered` state. The accepted operation-scoped observations and allowed embedded, referenced or hybrid façade views retain their contract-specific meaning. An operational observation never supplies information lifecycle state or establishes an Outcome by itself. Preserve necessary facts for recovery, audit and provenance under the existing retention and disclosure rules. [Issued A16][task] · [OperationalMetadata DRP §§3–4][om-mapping] · [Mneme Adapter / Façade][facade] · [IU Metadata][metadata]

**Reconciliation and Continuum amendments.** The synchronous interaction, closed Outcome vocabulary and write durability already agree with A16. In a governed incorporation, clarify component ownership and the absence of a generic IU progress or recovery structure in the associated OperationalMetadata and view descriptions. Inventory existing required projections before changing any binding. An interpretation that suppresses a required façade fact or mandates exposing internal progress would need explicit reconciliation against OM-08 and the existing projection contract. Preserve original MI-06 authoritative read-back, which requires evidence linking the observed durable revision to the submitted write. A higher revision alone leaves the result unresolved. [IU Metadata][metadata] · [Mneme Adapter / Façade][facade] · [Original MI-06][interaction-residual] · [Continuum Outcome][outcome]

**Detailed design.** Define success and evidence thresholds per operation and provider. Specify handling of timeouts and lost replies, safe correlation and retry, and recovery within the existing component responsibilities. Identify each required observation's owner, subject, scope, encoding, validation, schema evolution, visibility, retention and recovery purpose. Review any contract that exposes a governed projection and keep detailed stage and Reason vocabularies with their owners. This work leaves the common Outcome and adjacent OperationalMetadataMask and itinerary obligations at their existing standing. [Interaction DRP §§4.9–4.11][interaction-outcomes] · [Mnemosyne persistence DRP §6][persistence-outcomes] · [OperationalMetadata DRP §§6–7][om-selection]

**Implementation and verification.** Once authorised, exercise Success, Failure and Indeterminate on the synchronous contracts, including successful full and Summary writes and the evidence for required multi-site durability. Exercise provider ACK without durable completion, site loss, partial writes, timeout or lost reply after a possible commit, authoritative read-back with and without sufficient correspondence, and the distinction between persistence outcome and Mneme consumer disposition. Verify correct authorised projections and recovery without requiring shared IU transaction stages. [RAD 1.1.24 DR-1][rad24] · [Mneme Cache Architecture][cache] · [Continuum Outcome][outcome]

## 5. Matters for Solution Architect review

1. **A13 — External hold boundary.** Confirm what externally authoritative hold determination Harmonia may rely on to discharge the accepted recognition and enforcement obligations. If the intended SEP boundary removes an existing Harmonia responsibility for recognising or respecting an applicable hold, explicitly disposition that change. The detailed exchange and storage design can follow that boundary decision. [Legal Hold Recognition][hold-recognition] · [Retention-Hold Respect][hold-respect]
2. **A14 — DELETE contract allocation.** Decide where a distinct logical DELETE is exposed within the existing governed contracts and which IU/content or revision it governs, including the scope for externally referenced Content. Preserve the current RETIRE operation and the Summary contract until that disposition. [GovernedWriter][writer] · [GovernedSummaryWriter][summary-writer] · [HCRL.004 DR-8][hcrl-delete]
3. **A14 — Scope of the physical-destruction prohibition.** Disposition whether the categorical Business IU rule also binds external offline disposal for asserted or durably recorded information. Resolve the scope of any exception explicitly while preserving legislation, retention policy and applicable holds. [Business IU, source-qualified lifecycle §3][iu-history]
4. **A15–A16 — Contract preservation.** Exact revision formats/comparisons, active-generation coordination and operational projections are detailed design. Return for a further disposition if that work requires changing an accepted revision selection or projection, weakening durability, or broadening access to internal observations. [RAD 1.1.24 DR-1][rad24] · [RAD 1.1.25 DR-4][rad25-revisions] · [OperationalMetadata DRP][om]

At a later governed review, carry A13–A16 and the application obligations into the applicable HCRL decisions, particularly DR-3, DR-5 and DR-8. HCRL.004 retains its actual pending standing, including the separate software candidate and Topic/TopicMask design reviews. A subsequent ADD would need exact element and contract correspondence with preserved identities and predecessor evidence. This draft is presented for Solution Architect review.
