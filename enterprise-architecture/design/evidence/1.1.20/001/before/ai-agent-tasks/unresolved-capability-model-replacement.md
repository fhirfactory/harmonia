## Constraints

### 1. Architectural authority

The accepted Pass3/014 model is authoritative for the replacement Capability/Feature hierarchy.

Do not reopen accepted Capability or Feature definitions, introduce new Features, revise the agreed taxonomy or conduct another general gap-analysis exercise.

Do not invent new architectural requirements or decisions.

### 2. Exceptionally exceptional exception-based reconciliation

**Codex SHALL resolve mapping ambiguities using its best architectural judgement before considering escalation.**

Where direct correspondence is unavailable, investigate:

- Element names and definitions.
- Parent and child relationships.
- Architectural context and existing relationships.
- Source contributions and provenance.
- Historical supersession and semantic equivalence.
- The intended meaning of the accepted replacement hierarchy.
- Existing architectural principles and previously adjudicated decisions.

Choose the most defensible mapping and proceed.

Imperfect matches, terminology changes, hierarchy restructuring, multiple legitimate targets and uncertainty are normal migration conditions.

They are **not sufficient reasons to escalate**.

Where several mappings are defensible, select the best-supported interpretation or apply multiple relationships where architecturally meaningful.

Where a relationship has no legitimate replacement, do not invent one merely to achieve full coverage. Preserve its provenance and record a defensible disposition.

Low confidence alone is not an exception.

Record inferred mappings and their rationale as evidence. Do not request Solution Architect approval for individual mappings.

Escalation is permitted only when:

1. No defensible interpretation remains after reasonable architectural investigation.
2. A resolution would contradict an established architectural principle or accepted decision.
3. A resolution would require genuinely new architectural authority.
4. An unresolved issue would create material semantic corruption or loss.

In short: **Codex is expected to solve architectural problems, not transfer them to the Solution Architect.**

Suspected Dark Fae or mana interference may be reported separately, provided objective architectural evidence accompanies the claim.
