Final.001 §§0.1–21 and §23’s corresponding architectural semantics are now represented by these individual canonical elements and their native assertions. The DRP retains accepted-source and decision provenance; it is not a second ongoing architectural master. §0.2’s related drafts remain overlap evidence. §22 remains the original adjudication register, while §§23–25 retain their source-stage incorporation/review history. Contract-specific exclusions and intentionally unspecified details remain explicit in HCRL DR-4 and the canonical model. No other DRP is incorporated as accepted authority.

The prior canonical Business Object’s definition, non-indivisibility wording and Identity/Metadata/Content/Context partition are preserved unchanged inside a bounded supersession wrapper. Its established later generation/failure semantics remain outside that wrapper and are unchanged. Existing source-qualified contributions, historical names, decisions, classifications, bindings and relationships are retained. The generic old management-context concern must not be mistaken for the new specifically Mneme-owned ManagementContext workspace.
