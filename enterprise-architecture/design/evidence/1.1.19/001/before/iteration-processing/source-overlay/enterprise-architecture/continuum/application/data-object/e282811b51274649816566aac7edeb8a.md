# Provenance

## Architectural content

Provenance is governed information describing where/how information arose and the
activities associated with its creation, use or progression. It preserves origin
and processing history without conferring Information Authority. This DataObject
represents the Provenance information concept already described by existing
information-governance assertions; it adds no competing lineage or Assurance concept.

**Management and persistence:** [Dokimasia](../application-component/d739c75dffe2453081735710b285f03b.md)
owns the Provenance Management framework and lifecycle. Originating components
and execution activities generate this information using the Dokimasia-provided
Provenance Client Library. [Mneme](../application-component/1af8a56358f7429fabfb57be66542a99.md)
provides governed information management and [Mnemosyne](../application-component/7985e647bf9e4ca3ba70faf9e3e4ae02.md)
provides authoritative durable persistence using existing mechanisms. No independent
Provenance persistence subsystem is established. Ownership, custody and physical
persistence remain distinct; reliability, durability and timely information management
remain governed by existing requirements, including AX-05/10/11.

**References:** Records reference relevant [InformationUnits](class/74ec609de1ae498fbf9d13e4ef8c6cbe.md),
[Pragma instances](27ad143e7c784e378556af8a57c438f7.md), execution activities and
[associated Context](76ded4866bf443dabc0849d68b215690.md). References to activity
performed by [Ergon](../application-component/49c9eb2bef0c47b8b5328621d5fc5479.md)
do not turn Ergon itself into an Information Resource. Information contents need
not be embedded. Existing identity, versioning, information-reference and governance
semantics remain authoritative; these associations prescribe no reference schema,
cardinality, execution-attempt identity or lifecycle state.

**Assurance:** Provenance is foundational evidence used by Dokimasia when evaluating
governed activities and information against applicable assurance obligations.
Observed activity/evidence, Provenance describing it, applicable assurance basis,
and assessment/findings/outcomes remain distinct. [Praxis](0f961462340348b290fd844357d54d67.md)
remains the assurance basis; [Journey](359b901df27f4b43b5a2ff14a8484e34.md) remains
the observed-progression evidence subject, without becoming a complete Provenance
graph. Provenance availability does not itself establish an assurance conclusion;
insufficient evidence remains explicit under REQ-FND-005. Originating-component
evidence generation gives it no authority to determine, suppress, manufacture or
alter its own Assurance outcome.

**Audit boundary:** [Kleio](../application-component/fefda110e2274884ad58906733606efd.md)
retains Audit information management and historical audit evidence. Provenance
and AuditEvents remain distinct; audit evidence may contribute to Assurance without
making Kleio the assessment engine. Existing meaningful-evidence and ephemeral-state
rules (AX-08/09) still apply; routine mechanics are not automatically durable evidence.
The existing finite Dokimasia assurance boundary and absence of a separate own-execution
AuditEvent are preserved, without establishing a recursive assurance requirement.

**Standing and unresolved detail:** The Task records agreed responsibilities;
this representation is an ADD candidate awaiting Solution Architect acceptance under
HCRL 1.1.19-001 DR-18/DR-19. Retry original versus linked new Pragma, execution-attempt
representation, synchronous/asynchronous reporting, publication acknowledgement and
recovery, detailed schemas/cardinalities/lifecycle states and detailed client-library
contracts/deployment remain unresolved. These questions are not prerequisites for
representing the agreed responsibilities. No FHIR representation, information family,
transport, API, database or deployment model is selected.
