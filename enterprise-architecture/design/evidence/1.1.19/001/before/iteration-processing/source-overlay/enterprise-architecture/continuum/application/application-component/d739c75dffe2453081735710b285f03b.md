# Dokimasia

## Architectural content

- **Role/features:** specialised Ponos-based execution component associated with Energeia
  coordinating independent governed assurance activity over a subject. Journey
  C35 is the common observed-progression evidence subject/substrate for Pathway
  and Pub/Sub
  assurance under U04/U05; this does not limit all assurance subjects to Journey.
  [U02](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#u02) settles the candidate specialisation relationship; actual
  Assurance Praxis evaluates available
  evidence against applicable basis and produces governed assurance information.
  [A04 §2](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#a04), [L31](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#l31). **Praxis is the assurance basis;
  Journey is the observed evidence subject** under U06. Pathway/Route expectations
  contribute through applicable Praxis rather than constituting the general basis.
- **Information:** evidence/context/basis and assurance assessment/finding/outcome
  areas are documented. Journey observations remain distinct from inferred
  interpretation and assurance findings. Dokimasia activity/findings/results are
  O&A classification candidates under U06; detailed Data Objects, identity and
  preservation remain unresolved. Candidate vocabulary is not promoted into an
  Information Family.
- **Activity/collaboration:** executes discrete Dokimasia Ergon C26 specialisations,
  including candidate AI Assurance Ergon C27. Reuses Ponos execution, Ergo/Ergon
  activity, Mneme Pragma Management, process-management, platform integration,
  reporting and observability. This is specialisation/reuse, not runtime containment
  or a parallel assurance execution platform.
- **Information/persistence:** corresponding Pragma specialisation semantics are
  required; assurance request/input and assurance result/finding are candidate
  roles whose names, schemas, cardinalities and lifecycle remain unresolved.
  Evidence-access/control independence, detailed workflow composition and durable
  structures remain CDG-08/09. Common framework reuse does not let the assured
  activity determine, suppress, manufacture or alter its own assurance outcome.
- **Exclusions:** evidence, workflow completion and operational status are not
  assurance; custody does not make Kleio the evaluator. Dokimasia does not manage
  operational subject escalation/remediation and is not an established Twin subtype.
  AI invocation confers no authority on the model/service. Dokimasia can manage
  its own assurance work while the subject retains its operational management.
- **Standing:** conceptual independence remains established by A04; execution
  and discrete activity responsibilities are adjudicated for the candidate by U02.
  Detailed AI architecture requires CDG-35 discovery. No upstream responsibility
  is reopened; detailed Praxis/Journey and assurance contracts remain unresolved.

## Candidate ADD — Provenance Management and Assurance

Dokimasia owns the Provenance Management framework, including management and
lifecycle of Provenance generated by governed Harmonia activities. This responsibility
is distinct from physical persistence. Originating components and execution activities
generate Provenance using the reusable Dokimasia-provided Provenance Client Library
for creation, relevant references and reporting/publication into the framework.
Provenance is governed through Mneme and durably persisted through Mnemosyne using
existing mechanisms; no separate persistence subsystem is established.

Provenance is foundational evidence for independent Assurance of governed activities
and information against applicable obligations. Observed activity/evidence, describing
Provenance, applicable basis and assessment/findings/outcomes remain distinct. Praxis
remains the assurance basis and Journey the observed-progression evidence subject;
Journey is not thereby a complete Provenance graph. Common Ponos execution,
Dokimasia Ergon, authorised AI Assurance Ergon, operational-management separation,
evidence-access/control independence and existing detailed-design qualifications
remain authoritative. The assured activity must not determine, suppress, manufacture
or alter its own outcome. Evidence availability alone is not an Assurance conclusion.

Kleio retains Audit information management and historical audit evidence; that evidence
may contribute to Assurance without making Kleio its engine. Provenance ownership
neither transfers Audit responsibilities nor conflates Provenance with AuditEvents.
Existing finite-assurance and no separate own-execution AuditEvent constraints remain.
Retry Pragma identity, attempt representation, synchronous/asynchronous publication,
acknowledgement/recovery, schemas/cardinalities/lifecycle states and detailed library
contracts/deployment remain unresolved. No transport, API or deployment is selected.

Agreed intent is recorded in the operative ADD Task; this contribution awaits explicit
Solution Architect candidate acceptance under HCRL 1.1.19-001 DR-18/DR-19. Prior
definitions, assertion standing and unresolved detailed-design matters are preserved.
