## Dokimasia Provenance Management and Assurance

### 1. Goal

### 2. Expected Outcome

The governed Architecture Continuum must explicitly represent the following agreed architectural decisions.

#### 2.1 Provenance Management Ownership

**Dokimasia owns the Provenance Management framework.**

This includes responsibility for the management and lifecycle of Provenance information generated by Harmonia's governed activities.

Ownership of Provenance Management is distinct from responsibility for its physical persistence.

#### 2.2 Distributed Provenance Generation

Dokimasia provides a reusable **Provenance Client Library** for use by Harmonia components and execution activities.

The library performs the common work associated with:

- Provenance creation.
- Capturing references to relevant information and activities.
- Reporting or publishing Provenance information into the Provenance Management framework.

The originating component generates Provenance through the client library.

Dokimasia remains responsible for the framework and management of the resulting information.

This does not establish a particular runtime transport, API or deployment model.

#### 2.3 Governed Provenance Persistence

Provenance information is governed information.

It is managed through Mneme and durably persisted through Mnemosyne, using Harmonia's existing governed information-management and persistence mechanisms.

No independent Provenance persistence subsystem is authorised.

The persistence mechanism must support the established architectural requirements for reliable, durable and timely information management.

#### 2.4 Provenance References

Provenance records reference the relevant:

- InformationUnits.
- Pragma instances.
- Execution activities and their associated context.

Provenance does not require embedding the contents of the information being acted upon.

Existing identity, versioning, information-reference and governance semantics must be preserved.

#### 2.5 Provenance and Assurance

**Provenance is foundational evidence for Harmonia's Assurance framework.**

Dokimasia uses Provenance to evaluate governed activities and information against applicable assurance obligations.

The architecture must preserve the distinction between:

1. Observed activity and evidence.
2. Provenance information describing that activity.
3. Applicable Assurance basis.
4. Assurance assessment, findings and outcomes.

Existing architectural semantics concerning Praxis, Journey and independent Assurance remain authoritative.

#### 2.6 Separation of Audit and Provenance

Audit and Provenance are related but distinct architectural concerns.

**Kleio** remains responsible for Audit information management and historical audit evidence.

**Dokimasia** owns Provenance Management and performs independent governed Assurance assessment.

Audit evidence may contribute to Assurance, but Kleio does not become the Assurance engine.

Provenance Management ownership does not transfer Audit information-management responsibilities from Kleio.
