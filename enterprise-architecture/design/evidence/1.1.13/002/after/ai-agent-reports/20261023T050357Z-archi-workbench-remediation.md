## Before and after workbench behaviour

The accepted 1.1.11 Exchange organisation put a canonical parent's identifierRef
on an item that also had child items. Archi treated the item as a folder and left
the canonical element at its native root. Its four Business ValueStreams also
caused a literal `III Business` branch beneath Strategy. Unanchored context-only
navigation could disappear, and repeated live refs would relocate canonical objects.

The new adapter gives each canonical parent a distinct folder and one canonical
leaf. Child navigation remains alongside that leaf. It emits native category
markers so context-only folders survive without requiring any live alias reference.
It separates the four ValueStreams' native placement from Business contextual
navigation and qualifies both sides explicitly. The canonical element metadata
retains the same complete logical coordinate, path and reference semantics.

[parent-placement.json](../workbench/parent-placement.json) records every one of
the 218 canonical IDs, Exchange IDs, old detached paths and new actual paths.
[verification.json](../workbench/verification.json) records the independently
checked counts. This is complete population coverage, not a sample or an empty
selection interpreted as success.

Strategy retains `II Strategy / II.1 Capabilities` with its Business Capabilities,
Business Enabling Capabilities and other accepted scopes. CT1/CT2/CT3 parents have
their canonical element inside their folder. A CT2 or CT3 leaf with no children
remains an ordinary element alongside the appropriate navigation descendants.

Application retains `IV Application / IV.1 Information Architecture / IV.1.1
Topics`. Care's folder contains Care itself and its existing child Topics, including
CareLocation, Encounter, EncounterContext, EncounterProgression and Episode.
All 11 Topic parents receive this treatment; unrelated DataObjects remain in
their existing contexts. No Class or new Topic semantics are inferred.

Motivation retains `I Motivation / I.1 Drivers`. The three formerly detached
Drivers — 24/7 Availability, Audit & PHI Privacy and Clinical Interoperability —
now sit inside their numbered folders. Their four Stakeholder reference contexts
remain visible, citing the original canonical Stakeholders and exact associations.
The canonical Stakeholders remain singly placed in their index, not inside the
Driver reference folders.

Cross-Cutting References is visible as an explicitly non-decompositional index
under each applicable native layer. Motivation has the ten canonical Stakeholder
index entries. Strategy has the 214 governed historical Capability identities.
The R coordinates, history/currentness qualifications, successor/predecessor
references and distinct canonical identity population are preserved. These are
primary index placements, not repeated canonical references elsewhere. No
Motivation ownership of historical Capabilities or Strategy ownership of
Stakeholders is implied.

The Business ValueStream context contains four clearly qualified folders with
canonical IDs, Exchange IDs and native-location documentation. Strategy contains
the actual four canonical ValueStreams beneath the explicit Business-navigation
reference folder. Their canonical `III.2.2.*` coordinates do not become Strategy
decomposition. All 590 generated navigation-only folders survive the actual
import, including Governance references, unpopulated authorised domain headings,
Stakeholder contexts and the Application System Map.

## Remaining limits and proposed HCRL decision items

No canonical organisation defect remains unexplained in the verified population.
Native layer roots are retained intentionally. Relations remain flat under the
native Relations root, outside this remediation. A contextual folder supplies
documented canonical coordinates and IDs, not a clickable live element alias.
Some source-only Governance references point to documents/directories rather
than architectural elements, as the accepted navigation requires. Empty Technology
and Implementation & Migration headings preserve authorised absence without
inventing architecture. Archi's visual sibling sorting does not define semantic
order. The native-folder UUIDs generated during import are not canonical IDs.

The Solution Architect must inspect the actual candidate workbench before
acceptance. Proposed HCRL matters are: confirm the implemented projection policy
and actual tree (parents, native layers, Business Behaviour, references/history,
Views); confirm publication/preservation/round-trip results and the bounded
future inspection safeguard; and adjudicate the disclosed supporting Minutiae,
including unavailable Task instruction-entry time and filename prefix.
No new architectural decision about canonical containment, allocation, history,
Stakeholder associations or View organisation is requested or implied.

The pending HCRL records these outcomes and limits. It is not completed, and
this Report does not assert Solution Architect acceptance.
