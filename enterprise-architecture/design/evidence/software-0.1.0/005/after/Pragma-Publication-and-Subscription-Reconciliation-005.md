## Design agreement in the candidates

- **Work identity.** Pragma represents archetypal work available for subscription. Pragmatosis identifies the subscriber-specific undertaking and has its own identity, lifecycle, outcome and lineage. Stimulus → Noesis expresses the Digital Twin specialisation. [Publication/subscription candidate][draft]
- **One working environment.** Mneme contextualises and manages Pragmaton for a particular realised undertaking. Ergon.WorkSpace is the Ergon-facing access abstraction for that same environment. ContextLoader establishes initial working information with Mneme. ProcessingEngine works through WorkSpace. Ergon-InformationAdapter mediates further governed acquisition through Mneme. Themis PEP/PDP controls govern initial and later access. Specialised loaders can use different starting information while sharing this model. [Pragmaton candidate][pragmaton]
- **Associative access and authority.** The JavaSpaces analogy describes identity-based and associative access and governed lazy resolution during execution. The accepted Standard Cache supports summary/reference access, and the Workflow Cache supports transient local materialisation. Pragma and realisation summaries are immutable, content-free, non-authoritative snapshots. Contextualised values retain source and version lineage, and authoritative changes proceed through Mneme/Mnemosyne. [Pragmaton candidate][pragmaton] · [Two-cache acceptance][caches] · [IU revisions][iu]

These statements record design agreement at candidate level. Read the naming and responsibility questions retained in Pragmaton Draft.000.003 §9 in light of the follow-up instruction: exact candidate acceptance, canonical correspondence and detailed boundaries remain pending. AX-05, AX-06 and AX-09 preserve the durable-state, authority and transient-state boundaries. AX-07 governs each access. AX-14 and AX-15 preserve distinct identities and outcomes, including unresolved access or write outcomes. AX-17 governs adoption and any conflict with accepted authority. ## Outstanding architectural decisions

- **Formal Pragmaton association and lifecycle.** Confirm canonical representation and whether any linkage to the archetypal Pragma must be durable. Decide the minimum identity and revision semantics for the working environment and consistency guarantees across successively acquired values. The candidate already places WorkSpace in the realisation's execution context.
- **Interaction outcomes.** Confirm local associative matching versus permitted acquisition scope, admission of newly acquired values versus a bounded result, revocation or authority changes during execution, non-disclosure and unavailable outcomes, and save/unload evidence and persistence outcomes. These are governed contract decisions. Detailed interface shape remains software design.
- **Inherited DR-12 matters.** Preserve the existing publication gate and filtered-state questions, historical task and outcome mappings, parcel and selector boundary, SummaryCache allocation, retry/acknowledgement and Assurance evidence, and Digital Twin, Pylai and Dokimasia correspondence. Preserve the outstanding trust and Topic compatibility obligations in DR-7 and DR-10. [DR-12][review] · [DR-7][dr7] · [DR-10][topic-review]

## Conformance cases for candidate review

These are architectural review and future verification cases. The required outcomes express the design without selecting a schema, algorithm or transaction mechanism.

| Case | Evidence to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Ergon produces zero outputs or a mix of eligible and filtered outputs | Zero outputs need no downstream creation. Each filtered IU retains governed identity and a known normal disposition with no Pragma or subscription match. Eligible outputs have their own originating Pragma and one input. |
| Eligible Pragma yields zero matches or multiple matches | Zero matches remain observable apart from filtering. Multiple matches create independent undertakings, including two matches for distinct subscriptions of one subscriber. |
| Content cannot be materialised and governed summaries are available | Selection can use only permitted Pragma and input attributes. The subsequent Ergon read independently reports its governed unavailable outcome if Content is required. |
| Themis denies or cannot establish permission | Selection does not authorise disclosure, execution or release. A denied or unavailable authority decision has its own evidence and outcome. |
| WorkSpace lazily resolves a reference during execution | The adapter mediates the request through Mneme under applicable Themis controls. A denied or unavailable result remains distinguishable from an authorised materialisation. A content-free input snapshot retains its identity and revision correspondence. |
| Ergon changes an existing working value and generates a new output IU | The changed value returns to Mneme through governed save, with any authoritative version established by Mnemosyne. The new IU follows the independent PublishingMask path. |
| Partial fan-out or ambiguous persistence/handoff is retried | Confirmed undertakings retain identity across replays, including concurrent retries. Already-established work is not recreated. Persisted, handed-off and indeterminate outcomes are independently accounted for, with authoritative persistence preceding acknowledged durable handoff. |
| One realisation completes and another fails or is redelivered | Each subscriber-specific outcome and retry remains independently traceable to the originating Pragma, source IU revision, subscription and handoff. WorkSpace loss reconstructs required information from governed sources and durable work. |
| Specialised execution and independent assurance | Verify fresh Twin context and serial execution, Pylai ingress and egress boundaries, and Dokimasia's comparison of expected and observed progression with independent access to sufficient evidence. |
