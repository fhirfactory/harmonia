# G03 Pragma family cross-document reconciliation · Iteration 005

**Basis** · Architecture 1.1.26 · Software 0.1.0 review at Snapshot-004 (`6b058c1b332ac6244e6ff2d6aecad9d6c9e2a48d`) with the five worktree candidates identified below. [Review Task][task] · [Cumulative HCRL.004 / DR-12][hcrl]

## Executive reconciliation result

The five drafts describe one coherent core model. Mneme manages originating work and one distinct realisation per matching subscription. Petasos conveys a reference to the durably established undertaking in a by-value parcel. Ergon resolves and executes that undertaking using the Mneme-managed Pragmaton through WorkSpace. Authoritative persistence, handoff, delivery, execution and save retain independent meanings and evidence. The five drafts are ready for **collective Solution Architect adjudication with the exceptions below**. Acceptance of the exact family requires explicit treatment of the inconsistent Twin execution wording, the scope of the publication gate, and the material differences and contract gaps.

The existing Iteration 005 reconciliations preserve the prior decisions and obligations. This review brings those obligations together and identifies the bounded cross-document corrections. The five reviewed candidates retain their original bytes and pending standing. ## Proposed bounded corrections and remaining decisions

1. **Align the affected draft passages.** Treat G03-PF-01, 02, 05 and 12 in one scoped editing pass after authorisation. Preserve the agreed work roles, WorkSpace access relationship, cache allocation and PS-01–PS-16 decision history. Keep older drafts and source references available as lineage.
2. **Adjudicate semantic incorporation.** Resolve the reference target and pre-handoff establishment, work/outcome and single-input correspondence, publication eligibility placement and specialised Twin mapping before applying a dependent Continuum delta. Reuse governed identities and document any explicit qualification or deferral.
3. **Adjudicate the minimum owning contracts.** Use G03-PF-05–11 to identify the decisions needed for a dependent handoff, selector, scheduling, access/save, trust or Assurance contract. Retain existing DR-7, DR-10 and G03 A06/A07 obligations. Confirm the scope and ownership of any deferral before dependent work.

**Software design.** Once the relevant semantic contracts are decided, leave Java/API shapes, DTO and parcel encodings, indexing, cache-entry policy within accepted constraints, timer and retry mechanics, checkpoint representation, storage schema and deployment mechanics within existing mandates to solution design. These choices supply no further architectural exception.
