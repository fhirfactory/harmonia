IngressEndpoint → H01 Work receipt → Ergon.PEP.In → H02 Admission decision → Context Loader → H03 Context available → Ergon.Process → H04 Processing result → Output extraction → H05 Outputs returned → Filter (meaning open) → H06 Filter disposition → Ergon.PEP.Out → H07 Release decision → Petasos intake → H08 Petasos disposition

## Checkpoint, persistence and Assurance findings

- **Scope and lifecycle.** The accepted Features require defined checkpoint boundaries, durable required checkpoints, identifiable valid recovery state and evidence of completeness. The common Ergon framework owns checkpoints and execution recovery, Ponos coordinates execution and Petasos owns transport redelivery/recovery. Recovery restarts from the appropriate existing execution boundary with authoritative information and durable work. Cache presence and a completed Camel step supply working or operational evidence only. The checked sources leave the per-hop names, identity, lifecycle, durable record shape, retry/attempt correlation and mandatory checkpoint set unresolved. [Checkpoint Boundary Definition][checkpoint-boundary] · [Durable Task Checkpoint][checkpoint-durable] · [Recoverable Checkpoint Integrity][checkpoint-integrity] · [Checkpoint Completeness Evidence][checkpoint-completeness] · [1.1.24 DR-3][cache] · [Ponos][ponos]
- **Handover evidence, if captured.** Attribute H01 to Ergon receipt, H02 and H07 to the Ergon-hosted Themis decisions, H03 to the Loader, H04 to the actual Ergon execution, H05 to the Ergon return boundary, H06 to the adjudicated filter owner and H08 to the particular Petasos operation confirmed. These attributions identify sources for observations. Checkpoint identity and selection for durable recovery remain open.
- **Implementation names.** `ErgonBase` records `INGRESS` and `EGRESS`. `PraxisCheckpointManager` uses `PIPELINE_INGRESS`, `PRE_ERGON`, `POST_ERGON`, `PIPELINE_COMPLETE` and `PIPELINE_FAILED`. `PragmaCheckpoint` has a generated ID and carries workflow/step context. The manager's cache writes and logged failures leave authoritative recovery and mandatory audit durability unestablished. These are names and behaviours in the current code. [ErgonBase][ergon-code] · [PraxisCheckpointManager][checkpoint-code] · [PragmaCheckpoint][pragma-checkpoint-code]
- **Granularity and audit.** `COARSE`, `MEDIUM`, `FINE` and `EXTREME` are proposed. Diagnostics can describe machinery. Under AX-07/08/09, significant occurrences and required recovery state determine audit and persistence obligations independently of diagnostic volume. Clients report auditable occurrences. Kleio constructs authoritative immutable AuditEvents under its capture authority and the affected operation fails closed when required capture is unavailable. The pending Petasos–Kleio broker-staging design leaves the authoritative capture gate in force. [AX-07–09][axioms] · [Kleio, capture authority][kleio] · [Auditability and availability][kleio-durable] · [Petasos–Kleio §§3–5][audit-draft]
- **Independent Assurance.** Ponos, Ergon and Petasos contribute attributable operational observations; Energeia manages Journey. Dokimasia independently assesses available evidence against applicable Praxis and may use Kleio evidence. Observations, provenance, AuditEvents and findings retain their meanings. The assured activity cannot determine or suppress its own finding. Dokimasia Ergon reuses common execution facilities and follows the finite assurance and self-audit rules. Per-handover binary results and a universal independent capture hook are proposals. A binary verdict may summarise a known result under a defined rule. The evidence must retain denial, unavailability, no-match, indeterminate and unobserved outcomes when material. [Journey][journey] · [1.1.20 DR-19][assurance] · [Dokimasia][dokimasia] · [Finite boundary][finite] · [Self-audit rule][self-audit] · [AX-14/15][axioms]
- **Camel and CDI.** The checkout pins Camel **4.4.2**. Inspection of its `RouteDefinition`, `OutputDefinition` and `ProcessorDefinition` API found `process(Processor)` and `to(…)`, with no stock fluent `checkpoint()` in that route API. Camel component-specific checkpointing (for example Azure Event Hubs position tracking) has its own consumer semantics. Treat both checkpoint forms in the Task as Harmonia pseudocode. `direct:` invokes an in-context consumer synchronously and `seda:` queues in-process asynchronously without durable recovery. The approved **Exclusive PetasosParcel Routing** mandate requires Artemis for every PetasosParcel, including co-located participants. Current `ErgonBase` wires ingress, activity and egress methods into a Camel route. The Ponos code registers an injected `@Dependent` builder and uses `@ApplicationScoped` processors. CDI `@Dependent` follows the owning injection/lookup lifecycle. An instance injected into a route builder can serve multiple concurrent exchanges. The architectural constraint is execution isolation. The per-function CDI scope and concurrency design are open. Any implementation must isolate mutable execution state across concurrent Ergon instances, define processor ownership and destruction if looked up per execution, and preserve authorised concurrency, timeout and retry behaviour, including serial execution within one Twin. ## Discrepancies for Solution Architect disposition

1. **Filter subject and ownership.** Resolve whether the candidate slot denotes **PublishingMask evaluation** or **dead-task blocking**, and account for both responsibilities where applicable. Qualified Publishing applies a Pattern to egress Pragma under Mneme. G03 applies a producing Ergon's mask to a newly created IU before originating Pragma construction, with Ponos owning eligibility and Mneme evaluating. The exact timing/contract for context return, save, publication evaluation and release remains to be agreed. The separate Ponos Praxis selection API is still under review. [Pragma Management][publishing-base] · [Publication §6][publication-open] · [Routing §2.1][egress] · [Routing §4][praxis-router]
2. **Handover, continuation and outcome.** The qualified model assigns subscription selection to Mneme, references originating work in the parcel, creates the undertaking at the receiver and describes Ponos/Ergo output and continuation routing. G03 puts matching and fulfilment with Petasos, establishes the Pragmatosis before injection for subscription work, and has the Praxis router ask Ponos to select existing originating Pragmas. Reconcile the direct Praxis parcel/work realisation and the canonical distinction between G03 per-Ergon TaskOutcome and the existing TaskOutcome Topic for ActionableTask resolution. Define which acceptance or evidence owns H08 before using it to acknowledge or recover work. [Petasos][petasos] · [TaskOutcome Topic][task-outcome] · [Pragma Model §4.8][outcomes] · [Routing §5.1][routing-recovery] · [Routing §§7–7.1][routing-open]
3. **Delegation across enforcement boundaries.** G01 carries only the calling agent’s ID and leaves authority resolution with Themis. The source-qualified Themis account uses Identity/PEP-Criteria pass-off terminology. G01 §9 leaves the representation and trusted transport binding open. Preserve the ID-only G01 rule and distinct denied/unavailable outcomes while the correspondence is settled. [G01 §9][g01-open] · [Themis Availability and delegation][themis-delegation]
4. **Names and evidence boundaries.** Retain **IngressEndpoint/EgressEndpoint**, `Ergon.PEP.In`, `Ergon.Process`, `Ergon.PEP.Out` and **Context Loader / Context Unloader**. Confirm the G03 spellings and the narrower meanings of output extraction, filtering and Petasos intake before naming additional functions. Use **handover point** for H01–H08 pending the selection and identity of governed checkpoints. If the term **Route** is used for this depiction, qualify it as a Camel or logical processing route. The qualified architecture calls the Ergon's internal passage a **PathwayTraversal** and the ordered inter-component segments a **Route**. Decide the minimum separately attributable operational observation, diagnostic, durable recovery, Kleio capture and independent Assurance evidence for applicable handovers before approving any granularity scheme.
