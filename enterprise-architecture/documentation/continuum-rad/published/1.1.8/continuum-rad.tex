\chapter*{Reading the Architecture Continuum}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Reading the Architecture Continuum}
Start with Motivation for architectural intent and axioms, then Strategy for
capabilities and Business for operational responsibilities. Application describes
software responsibilities and managed information. The chapters follow the
complete governed model organisation; folder nesting is navigation, not an
additional architectural relationship. The existing model's Continuum additions
branches retain their labels, order and nesting within the applicable native EA
domain Part. No fallback chapters or independent RAD hierarchy are introduced.

The Architecture Continuum was accepted as the canonical baseline in RAD-1.1.7.
This acceptance preserves the separate standing of each assertion. In particular,
currentness does not mean approval. Candidate and Proposed descriptions remain
qualified; source wording about pending detailed architecture is retained.
Historical and superseded elements remain qualified within their existing model
branches, with links to their canonical definitions. They are included in the
complete correspondence record.

Use the linked architectural names and page index to move between responsibilities.
Each entry identifies its standing and links to canonical Markdown. Chapter
introductions offer source-cited reading guidance. Explanatory diagrams show
selected, explicitly represented relationships, with their existing qualifications;
layout and proximity assert nothing further. Detailed source contributions,
provenance, independent naming/placement qualifications and recorded unresolved
properties remain available through those links and the Continuum Manifest.

The \href{../../../../design/register/1.1.7/hcrl-1.1.7-001.complete.md}{accepted baseline record}
and \href{../../../../governance/architectural-axioms.md}{Architectural Axioms}
retain their governed roles. The superseded LaTeX RAD supplies reused typesetting
facilities only. Its model copies and semantic extracts are not publication inputs.
The \href{../../../continuum-manifest/published/1.1.8/continuum.html}{Continuum Manifest browser}
provides the full structural catalogue. The machine-readable \href{structure.json}{publication correspondence}
records every source's placement and preserved qualifications.
