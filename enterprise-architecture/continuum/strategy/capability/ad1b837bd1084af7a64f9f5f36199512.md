# [CT3] Ergon Output Processing & Continuation

## Architectural content

Process Ergon execution outcomes and determine their consequences for the enclosing Task, including continuation, subsequent work, failure handling and correlation of outstanding activities. Preserve and reconcile task-publication and subscription obligations through subscriber loss, redelivery, partition and restart. Ergon completion is not necessarily activity completion; activity completion is not necessarily Task completion; Task completion is not necessarily authoritative information commitment. Long-duration activities do not automatically require additional Tasks, and activity semantics are independent of a particular Stimulus, Effector or implementation mechanism.

Catalogue identity `E06.08.07`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
