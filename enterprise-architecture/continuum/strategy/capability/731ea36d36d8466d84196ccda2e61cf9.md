# [CT3] Clinical Device Maintenance Management

## Architectural content

Definition unresolved in the baseline. No definition has been synthesised from the name.

**Proposed overview**

Maintenance/restoration is materially distinct from overall lifecycle disposition and monitoring; include the Candidate concern as a proposed eighth CT3.

**Proposed definition**

Maintain or restore acceptable device condition through testing, calibration, inspection, maintenance and repair.

**Proposed boundary**

The element remains Candidate. The definition/placement elaboration is Proposed and does not adjudicate the unresolved RAD question or transfer source-domain authority.

---

## History

### Capability Overview — predecessor explanation

This retained concern addresses maintaining or restoring acceptable device condition through testing, calibration, inspection, repair and related maintenance activity. Its purpose is distinguishable from observing device condition and deciding overall lifecycle disposition. The definition is useful architectural context, but the explicit seven-child device set omits this concern.

### Boundary — predecessor explanation

Membership, tier and ancestry require adjudication; this description does not establish an eighth Clinical Technology Management child.
