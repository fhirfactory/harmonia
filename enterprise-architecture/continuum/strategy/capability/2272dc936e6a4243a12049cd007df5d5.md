# [CT3] Routing Decision Implementation

## Architectural content

Apply governed PetasosParcel routing decisions, including destination selection and preservation of the applicable rules, rationale, decision context and qualifications. Destination selection is distinct from a delivery attempt and from accepted handoff of responsibility. Routing does not take ownership of queue delivery, work execution or payload interpretation, and a successful decision does not establish delivery or acceptance by the destination.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `E06.03.03`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
