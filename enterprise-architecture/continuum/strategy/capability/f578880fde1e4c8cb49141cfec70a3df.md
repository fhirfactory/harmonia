# [CT3] Long-Duration Asynchronous Activity Management

## Architectural content

Maintain continuing activities within their applicable Task context, including activity state, correlation, progress and eventual outcomes. Recover outstanding real-world obligations despite lost clients, offline periods, inconsistent reports or Digital Twin restart. Ergon completion, activity completion, Task completion and authoritative information commitment remain distinct outcomes. Long duration does not automatically require an additional Task, and activity semantics are independent of a particular Stimulus, Effector or implementation mechanism.

Catalogue identity `E06.09.07`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
