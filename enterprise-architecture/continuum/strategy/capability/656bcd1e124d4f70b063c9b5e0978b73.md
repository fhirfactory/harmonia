# [CT3] Clinical Handover Acceptance

## Architectural content

Clinical responsibility is explicitly accepted by an accountable, appropriately identity-assured receiving party, with sufficient transferred care context, ownership of outstanding obligations, and traceable acceptance evidence and timing. Receipt alone does not establish acceptance; rejection, delay and escalation remain explicit. Clinical accountability remains distinct from governed operational responsibility transfer and human operational handover. This Business capability does not itself assign clinical decision or handover authority to Harmonia.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `B05.03.01`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
