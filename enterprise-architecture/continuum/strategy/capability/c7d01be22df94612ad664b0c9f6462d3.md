# [CT3] Governed Parcel Handoff & Accountability

## Architectural content

Govern the transfer of PetasosParcel responsibility between processing participants to the destination prescribed by a governed routing decision. Preserve handoff state, accountable participants, outcome correlation and applicable audit evidence. Destination selection, delivery attempt and accepted handoff are distinct; a routing decision or delivery attempt alone does not establish acceptance of responsibility. Undeliverable Parcels retain an explicit disposition.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `E06.03.01`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
