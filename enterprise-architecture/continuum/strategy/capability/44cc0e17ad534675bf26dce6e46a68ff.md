Information administration does not confer authority over the represented person, organisation, service, place, device or clinical activity. Default-deny authority evaluation, consent constraints, security-context propagation and security-significant evidence have distinct purposes: deciding permission, retaining restrictions, carrying attributable operational context and preserving required evidence. The capability makes security intrinsic to the operation rather than optional presentation behaviour.

### Boundary — predecessor explanation

Security context is operational context and does not automatically become resource content. Security-context validation has no invented one-to-one mapping to propagation, and audit recording is not an assurance conclusion.

### Harmonia Architectural Relevance — predecessor explanation

Assigned policy enforcement and attributable context are substantive Core responsibilities across managed operations.
