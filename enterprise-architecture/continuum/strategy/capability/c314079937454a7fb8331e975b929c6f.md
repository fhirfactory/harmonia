# [CT3] Review & Confirmation Interaction

## Architectural content

Review & Confirmation Interaction supports review and explicit confirmation/rejection of intended actions by both authorised programmatic participants and human participants, according to their distinct interaction and authority requirements. It does not presume that every confirmation requires a human or that automated confirmation is equivalent to human authority.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `E07.02.02`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
