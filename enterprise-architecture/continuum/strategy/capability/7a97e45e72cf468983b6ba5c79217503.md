# [CT2] Asynchronous Digital Twin–Entity Work Coordination

## Architectural content

Preserve governed work across the digital/physical boundary: a Twin may delegate or reflect a real-world undertaking, remain able to progress independent stimuli and subsequently reconcile outcomes without a continuously executing Ergon.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `E06.09`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
