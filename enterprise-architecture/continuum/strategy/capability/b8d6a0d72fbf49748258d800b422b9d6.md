# [CT3] Machine-to-Machine Trust Continuity

## Architectural content

Services and workloads authenticate across the boundary between Harmonia and external systems, maintaining valid machine-to-machine trust and rejecting expired or revoked trust without conflating workload identity with human business authority.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `E05.01.04`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
