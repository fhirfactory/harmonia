# [CT3] Participant-Contextual Information Protection

## Architectural content

Participant information is protected according to context during authorised access, presentation and disclosure, with applicable consent, authorisation and disclosure conditions preserved. Contextual protection does not necessarily require de-identification. Safeguarding concerns, including abuse or neglect, can be identified, safely reported, reviewed, acted upon and followed up while respecting autonomy. This Business capability does not imply that Harmonia performs every underlying transformation or makes every disclosure decision.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `B14.06.01`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
