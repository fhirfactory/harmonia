# [CT3] Health Service Operational Readiness

## Architectural content

Definition unresolved in the baseline. No definition has been synthesised from the name.

**Proposed overview**

Collective readiness is an access prerequisite and has a coherent proposed preparedness frame; it is not reduced to capacity, resource state or service alignment.

**Proposed definition**

Establish collective operational readiness of a service offering in a site or session context from its applicable dependencies.

**Proposed boundary**

The element remains Candidate. The definition/placement elaboration is Proposed and does not adjudicate the unresolved RAD question or transfer source-domain authority.

---

## History

### Capability Overview — predecessor explanation

This candidate concern asks whether an identifiable health-service offering can actually be delivered from its operational context. Its meaning is collective: workforce, places, equipment, materials, utilities and other dependencies must be considered together without acquiring their ownership. It explains why a defined offering, available practitioner or ready bed alone cannot establish service readiness.

### Boundary — predecessor explanation

Tier, parent and CT1 ancestry remain unresolved. Site-level and session-level exercises do not establish separate capabilities, and no convenient parent is allocated here.
