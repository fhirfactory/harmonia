# [CT3] Research Information De-identification & Minimisation

## Architectural content

Research-purpose information can be prepared through appropriate de-identification, pseudonymisation and minimisation, preserving research utility while managing re-identification risks. Participants' rights, information and wellbeing remain protected through consent, risk monitoring, withdrawal recognition and adverse-event response. De-identification does not authorise unrestricted disclosure: consent, authorisation and disclosure conditions remain governed. This Business capability does not imply that Harmonia performs every underlying transformation or makes every disclosure decision.
