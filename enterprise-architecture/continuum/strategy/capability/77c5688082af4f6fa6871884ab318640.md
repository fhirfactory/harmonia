# [CT3] Trusted System Interaction

## Architectural content

Internal Harmonia systems, components and automated principals can authenticate to the services they are authorised to use, establishing valid identity and trust and rejecting expired or revoked trust.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `E05.01.02`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
