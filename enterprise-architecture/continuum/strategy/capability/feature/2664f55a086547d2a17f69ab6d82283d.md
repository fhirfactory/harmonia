# [Feature] RepeatedOfferRecognised

## Architectural content

Redelivery and repeated offers do not silently create duplicate accepted undertakings. Repeated Ergon outcomes are correlated with their Task and outstanding activities so that continuation does not silently duplicate those undertakings.
