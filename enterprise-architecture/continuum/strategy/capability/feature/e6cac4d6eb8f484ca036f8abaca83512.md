# [Feature] Applicable Obligation Identification & Mapping

## Architectural content

Applicable statutory, regulatory, contractual and other authoritative obligations are identified for a governed service or enterprise context and mapped to their authoritative sources, relevant jurisdictions, effective scope and versions, and the contexts to which they apply, with accountable interpretation where required.
