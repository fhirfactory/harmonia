# [Feature] Accountable Critical-Result Recipient

## Architectural content

Critical diagnostic findings are directed to an appropriately accountable intended recipient under the applicable clinical responsibility arrangements, with sufficient recipient identity assurance. The responsibility basis can be established without Harmonia assuming authority to assign clinical responsibility.

### Incorporation standing

Catalogue identity `B04.03.04.F01`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
