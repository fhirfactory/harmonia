# [Feature] Protocol Acknowledgement Correlation

## Architectural content

Each protocol-level acknowledgement can be associated with its corresponding transaction or interaction, preserving the identity and context required to establish that relationship. A protocol acknowledgement alone does not establish application or logical processing success.
