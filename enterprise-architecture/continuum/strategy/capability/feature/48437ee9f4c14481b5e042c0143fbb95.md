# [Feature] Programmatic Action Confirmation

## Architectural content

An authorised software component or automated participant, including a Digital Twin or agentic participant where permitted, can explicitly confirm or reject an intended action through a correlatable programmatic interaction, with the action, confirmation disposition and attributable supporting evidence distinguishable from transport delivery or completed execution.
