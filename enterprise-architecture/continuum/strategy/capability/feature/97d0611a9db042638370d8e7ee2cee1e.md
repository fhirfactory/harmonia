# [Feature] Pre-Processing Work Preservation Assurance

## Architectural content

As accepted work is prepared for processing, its existing durable preservation and the recoverability of required processing state are checked and established for supported ordinary component or node failures. This assurance does not require a second persistence of already preserved work and does not establish catastrophic recovery.
