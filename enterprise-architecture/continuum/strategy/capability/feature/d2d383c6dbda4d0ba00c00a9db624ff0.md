# [Feature] Confirmed Receiving Responsibility

## Architectural content

Care transfers have explicit, evidenced acceptance of clinical responsibility by an accountable receiving party. Handover identifies who accepted responsibility; receipt notification alone does not establish acceptance without the required evidence.
