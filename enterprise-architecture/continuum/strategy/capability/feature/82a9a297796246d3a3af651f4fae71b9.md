# [Feature] Attributable Critical-Result Communication Evidence

## Architectural content

Critical-result communication retains attributable evidence of the finding, intended and actual recipient, delivery, acknowledgement timing and applicable follow-up. Delivery, acknowledgement and clinical action remain distinguishable.
