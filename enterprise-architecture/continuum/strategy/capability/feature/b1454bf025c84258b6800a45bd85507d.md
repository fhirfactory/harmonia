# [Feature] Native Representation Conformance

## Architectural content

The structural and conformance characteristics of a retained source-native information representation can be identified and evaluated against the applicable declared native format or standard, while preserving the distinction between the representation as received and its conformance status. A received representation need not be conformant in order for its original native form to be retained.
