# [Feature] Routing Decision Evidence

## Architectural content

Each governed routing decision records sufficient information to establish its selected destination, applicable rules or criteria and rationale, including decision context and qualifications. The decision can be correlated with actual movement without treating a selected route as proof of delivery.
