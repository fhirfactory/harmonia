# [Feature] Evidence Assembly Completeness and Qualification

## Architectural content

Assembled provenance evidence identifies its required constituents, relationships and completeness, preserving attribution, chronology and qualifications for unavailable evidence. Assembly completeness is distinguished from the portion permitted for disclosure.
