# [Feature] Rejected Handover Resolution

## Architectural content

Rejected handovers receive an explicit resolution, maintained through relevant changes or disruptions and recovered where required. Rejection, missing acceptance or delayed acceptance prompts escalation under the applicable clinical handover arrangements without falsely completing the transfer of responsibility.
