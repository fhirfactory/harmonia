# [Feature] Twin Operational Context Recoverability

## Architectural content

The operational context of each Digital Twin instance, including its governed work state and associated information, remains recoverable following complete failure of the Digital Twin execution engine, independently of the continued availability of that engine.

Catalogue identity `E06.02.05.F04`. The accepted definition is preserved above. Migration verification and source measurements do not establish implementation satisfaction. Existing source qualifications remain in force.
