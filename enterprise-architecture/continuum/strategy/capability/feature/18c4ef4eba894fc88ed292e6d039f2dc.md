# [Feature] End-to-End Acknowledgement Correlation

## Architectural content

All acknowledgements associated with a governed transaction can be identified and correlated across applicable protocol, application and logical processing or commitment layers, including multiple acknowledgements relating to the same transaction, with their distinct meanings preserved.
