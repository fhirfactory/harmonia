# Establish, exercise and revise consent choices

## Architectural content

An evidenced need to establish, exercise and revise consent choices arises. Progresses towards: Applicable consent, refusal, restrictions and changes can inform permitted activities and remain explainable and attributable.

### Alternative outcomes and conditions

- The request may be declined, rejected or held for further evidence under the applicable source conditions.
- An uncertain state or incomplete prerequisite does not establish success.

Progression may be conditional or long-running. A correction or review may revisit an earlier constituent. Independent work may progress concurrently only where its applicable prerequisites permit; this definition prescribes no scheduler or execution order.

This is a reusable Journey definition. Actual progress, decisions and outcomes belong to particular occurrences. It selects no workflow engine, orchestration, runtime state machine or execution owner.
