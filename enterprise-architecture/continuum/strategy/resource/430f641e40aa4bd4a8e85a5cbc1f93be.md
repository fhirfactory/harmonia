# National Clinical Terminology Assets (SNOMED CT-AU / AMT)

## Architectural content

#### Strategic Definition
**National Clinical Terminology Assets** comprise the authoritative, governed clinical vocabularies, concept identifiers, descriptions, and semantic relationships used to capture clinical meaning unambiguously across healthcare systems.

#### Enduring Strategic Role
Harmonia requires authoritative clinical vocabularies to govern semantic meaning during data ingestion, correlation, and egress:
- **SNOMED CT-AU**: The Australian extension to SNOMED CT, providing the clinical concept reference hierarchy for clinical findings, diagnoses, procedures, and body structures.
- **Australian Medicines Terminology (AMT)**: The national standard terminology for medicinal products, supporting unambiguous medication reconciliation, prescribing concepts, and dispensing data.

These terminology assets enable Harmonia to evaluate clinical semantic equivalence, enforce conformance rules, and execute meaning-preserving transformations without corrupting clinical intent.

*(Note on LOINC: While Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes (LOINC) is a recognised international standard for laboratory and diagnostic observations, its formal admission as an enduring foundational resource is governed by jurisdictional adoption and is not pre-empted ahead of explicit canonical scope).*
