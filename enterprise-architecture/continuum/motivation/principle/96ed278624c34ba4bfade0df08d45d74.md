# Governed PHI Logging & Dual-Logger Privacy Controls

## Architectural content

Operational log streams (INFO, WARN, ERROR) must remain strictly devoid of Protected Health Information (PHI), personally identifiable demographics, and security credentials under all conditions. Clinical payload data and diagnostic identifiers are permitted exclusively at DEBUG and TRACE severity levels via the dedicated PhiLogger dual-gate security evaluation mechanism, routing non-additively to isolated, encrypted diagnostic storage (documented in Appendix \ref{app:phi_logging}).
