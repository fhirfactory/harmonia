# Autonomous System

## Architectural content

Perspective of independently meaningful participation by software, AI agents, Digital Twins or other computational entities. Ordinary automated processing alone does not qualify. Its identity, authority and actual participation must be established in context.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
