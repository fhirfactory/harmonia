# External Organisation

## Architectural content

Perspective of an organisation participating in its own capacity independently of a particular individual. Organisational participation is not reducible to an employee or contact-person role.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
