# Patient (superseded 1.1.21 allocation)

## Architectural content

Perspective of an individual receiving, seeking or being the subject of healthcare. This perspective expresses care-related needs and consequences; it is not a Person superclass.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.

### Superseded allocation

The original text above records the initial ADD candidate and is retained as historical content. This allocation no longer represents another current catalogue perspective. Current Patient meaning and participation use canonical identity `5f6d333e94634420b8a8bbf8b9cb6e2a`. The original binding is reserved and never recycled; source correspondence and the moved relationship are recorded in identity replacement history and the bounded follow-up evidence.
