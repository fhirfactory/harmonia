# Participant

## Architectural content

Perspective of an individual taking part in health-information or care-related activity in their own capacity. Participation is contextual and does not imply that the individual is currently a Patient.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
