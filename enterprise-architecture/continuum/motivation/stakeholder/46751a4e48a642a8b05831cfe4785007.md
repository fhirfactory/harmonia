# Diagnostics

## Architectural content

Perspective of diagnostic services and participants contributing or using pathology, imaging and related diagnostic information. It does not transfer source clinical authority to Harmonia.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
