# Responsible Person

## Architectural content

Perspective of recognised responsibility for, or representation of, a Patient or Participant. It accommodates carers, guardians and other recognised representatives without implying automatic authorisation; applicable authority must be established separately.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
