# Administrators (Facility)

## Architectural content

Perspective of administrative participation in a healthcare facility, concerned with accurate administrative information and supported service arrangements. It is distinct from system administration.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
