# Operations (Facility)

## Architectural content

Perspective of those coordinating operational work and resources within a healthcare facility, including operational demand, handoffs and resolution. It does not determine physical deployment ownership.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
