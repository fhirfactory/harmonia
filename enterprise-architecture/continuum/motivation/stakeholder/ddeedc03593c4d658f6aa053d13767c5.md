# Administrators (System)

## Architectural content

Perspective of configuring and administering governed system facilities and administrative information. Administrative identity does not confer unrestricted access or clinical authority.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
