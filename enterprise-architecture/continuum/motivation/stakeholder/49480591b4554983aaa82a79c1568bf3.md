# Practitioner

## Architectural content

Perspective of a healthcare practitioner contributing professional information, judgement or interaction within the applicable care context. Professional scope and technical authorisation remain separate.

This is one of the sixteen accepted catalogue perspectives. It creates no organisational role hierarchy. Journey participation and authorisation are independent.
