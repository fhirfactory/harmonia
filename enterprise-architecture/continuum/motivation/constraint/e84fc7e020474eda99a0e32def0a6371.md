# Independent Audit Targets Are Not Assumed Replicas

## Architectural content

These targets SHALL NOT be described as replicas unless and until convergence/replication semantics are actually established. For R1.x/R2.x they are **independent authoritative audit persistence targets**.

This assertion is normalised from the governed DesignNote under U07/U15 and the ARB release Praxis. Original candidate/release qualifications and separately deferred implementation details remain in force.
