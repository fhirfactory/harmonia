# Kleio Constructs AuditEvent Representations for Clients

## Architectural content

> **Clients SHALL NOT be required to construct FHIR AuditEvent resources. Clients submit auditable occurrences through the Kleio Audit Capture contract. Kleio is responsible for constructing and managing the authoritative FHIR AuditEvent representation.**

This assertion is normalised from the governed DesignNote under U07/U15 and the ARB release Praxis. Original candidate/release qualifications and separately deferred implementation details remain in force.
