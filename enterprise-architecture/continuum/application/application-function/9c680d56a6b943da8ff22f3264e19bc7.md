# Mnemosyne Adapter (Store)

## Architectural content

### Approved 1.1.24 incorporation — resulting candidate pending

RequiredIU-Criterion and SupportedIU-Criterion are collections of shared TopicMasks describing required and supported IU types, independent of Themis criteria. **Every required IU type must be covered by the union of eligible available instances' supported criteria**; mere overlap is insufficient. Verify configured coverage at startup and during operation; missing expected instances/capabilities alarm. Enumerating current Topics alone does not prove an open-ended mask's complete coverage. Proof/validation algorithms remain solution detail; uncertain coverage cannot be declared complete (MI-05).

The concrete requested Topic must match the applicable required criterion and the selected available instance's supported criterion. Additional advertised support is permitted; no symmetric exact-match or per-operation discovery is required. Each IU-type registration covers all five logical contracts; native search expression support remains qualified.

Expected stable instance identities, sites and capabilities are configuration-defined. Registration verifies expectations and selects among existing eligible instances; it does not invent/relocate the deployment after site failure. A failed instance normally restarts in its configured context. Availability, declaration, permission and factual Outcome are distinct. Changeover preserves coherent authoritative logical IU identity/version/state or reports uncertainty (AV-02/04).

Expose exactly SummaryReader, SummaryWriter(CREATE, RETIRE), SummarySearch, full Reader and full Writer through the established participants. Origin-instance SummarySearch continuation cannot migrate. Reconcile uncertain potentially higher-version writes through authoritative GovernedReader(version=*) plus sufficient correspondence/durability evidence; preserve Indeterminate when insufficient. Mneme retains per-IU concurrency and consumer disposition; no new TopicMask language, transaction identifier, registry or selection protocol is introduced.


Mneme's persistence-facing adapter discovers IU-type capabilities registered by Mnemosyne-side Mneme Adapters / Façades, using the existing shared TopicMask primitive: element-wise regex matching against dot-separated canonical-name elements. It establishes eligible instances and selects among them for the request.

The distributed architecture must support (1) high availability and changeover when an instance becomes unavailable, (2) concurrent processing of distinct IU instances of the same registered IU type across multiple Mnemosyne instances, and (3) load balancing across eligible instances. Capability support and current availability are different facts. Identical, overlapping and disjoint registrations are supported; no one-to-one Store/instance mapping is imposed.

Mneme retains concurrency management for each individual IU. Selection of another Mnemosyne instance must reach the same logical authoritative persistence engine for that IU and preserve version/authority/security guarantees. Instance selection does not replace the IU's governed InformationCoordinates. No concurrent authoritative writes across independent engines are permitted.

Mnemosyne reports factual persistence Outcome; Mneme decides its own subsequent response. Mneme may treat Indeterminate as failure of its own operation without changing the reported persistence status. Changeover does not prove a previous in-flight write failed or make blind mutation retry safe. Availability detection, registration expiry, routing, load policy, failover protocol and coordination machinery remain solution-design responsibilities, with no mechanism selected by this incorporation.
