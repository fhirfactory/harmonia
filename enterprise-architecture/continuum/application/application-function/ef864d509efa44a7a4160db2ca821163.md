# InformationUnit Controller

## Architectural content

Mnemosyne's second responsibility tier controls governed IU operations and enforces applicable persistence-context obligations. It executes Themis-provided/enabled PDP and PEP functionality locally where required. Themis retains policy and authorisation semantics; distributed execution creates neither an independent Mnemosyne policy authority nor a compulsory call to a central PDP.

Applicable IU-required SecurityContext, Identity Passport/effective authority, compartment and information-change conditions remain distinct. A TopicMask capability match, InformationCoordinates match, native credential or prior Mneme decision does not by itself grant disclosure/mutation authority or replace applicable persistence-context enforcement. Security applies to search, result metadata, indexes, logs and errors as required. No mandatory persistence of transient security context or alternative policy framework is introduced.

### Incorporation standing

Accepted parent DRP semantics, incorporated under the operative Task for 1.1.23/001. Actual resulting candidate confirmation remains pending. Earlier component/source qualifications remain independently in force.
