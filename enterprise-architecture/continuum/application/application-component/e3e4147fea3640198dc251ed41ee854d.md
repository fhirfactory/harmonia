# Pylai

## Architectural content

- **Role/responsibility:** **Harmonia's External Interoperability Service**,
  enabling/managing system-to-system communication irrespective of style, protocol,
  standard or technology. Messaging, synchronous/asynchronous RMI, APIs, files and
  polling are supported architectural possibilities; RMI does not mean Java RMI.
  [U14](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#u14), [canonical Pylai/Horos view](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/pylai-external-interoperability.md).
- **Constructs/patterns:** Terma C43 acts at the logical external interface;
  Diadromí C44 carries/represents transient interaction. PylaiPathway connects
  governed Praxis/Ergo work; PylaiBroker serves executing BusinessLogic;
  PylaiServer changes use Pathway and READ/SEARCH uses Diadromí-Controller C45.
  These patterns do not mandate classes, services or deployment boundaries.
- **Information/persistence:** Communication Information Unit preserves raw payload,
  communication metadata and applicable external identity; exact schema unresolved.
  Ingress Loader establishes Task.input/Context, with semantic mapping in paired
  BusinessLogic. Durable acceptance requires authoritative Pragma/Task persistence
  through Mneme/Mnemosyne. Protocol ACK and queue/cache success are distinct.
  Diadromí is transient and non-recoverable; Petasos owns durable Parcel handoff.
- **Security/evidence:** carries external identity; Themis establishes Harmonia
  authority/enforcement. Kleio constructs authoritative AuditEvent from reported
  significant transactions; Dokimasia assures; Journey records applicable observed
  business progression or interoperability failure. None of these are synonyms.
- **Exclusions/standing:** no execution framework, persistence manager, authority
  inference, fabricated READ/SEARCH work or mandatory Controller for all interactions.
  AX-02/13 non-destructive fail-closed publication remains. Technology/handover
  details remain CDG-32/34/50; CDG-51 records the resolved Strategy boundary.
  Kleio mandatory capture uses its own governed platform authority, not the
  external/acting Identity's grants; Pylai gains no such authority.

## Source-qualified contribution

Source: `docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/pylai-external-interoperability.md` — ## Identity, authorisation, audit, assurance and Journey. Standing: Candidate — adjudicated Domain05 Step-B baseline; final Solution Architecture unapproved.

**Pylai carries external identity into Harmonia.** Trusted external Identity/context
is interpreted through [Identity.Authentication and Identity.Authorisation](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/component-register.md#identity-authentication).
Pylai SHALL NOT independently derive Harmonia authority, AuthorisationRoles,
Function permissions, Function.Scope or PEP-Criteria from claims. Untrusted
caller claims grant no authority. Authentication remains SEP; Themis owns
AuthorisationRoles, runtime Criteria, PEP-Criteria, policy resolution and the local
enforcement framework. Pylai hosts applicable Themis enforcement at operations
it controls, including Controller interactions. Detailed token/claim carriage
remains deferred; operational security context does not automatically become
persisted resource content or an externally publishable representation.

Architecturally meaningful interoperability transactions are **auditable**.
Pylai reports applicable transaction occurrence through Kleio's contract; Kleio
creates/manages authoritative audit information/AuditEvent. Low-level network
events do not each require an AuditEvent. Required authoritative durable capture
and [Kleio availability constraints](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/component-register.md#auditability-and-availability)
remain in force, independent of Journey participation.

Communication failures supply operational evidence and may be relevant to
Dokimasia. Pylai is neither the audit repository/policy manager nor the assurance
engine. **Audit is separate; audits are not governance.** Kleio performs mandatory
capture under [its governed intrinsic platform authority](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/component-register.md#kleio-capture-authority).
The external/acting Identity remains audit evidence, not the authority persisting
that evidence. This establishes no equivalent platform authority for Pylai.

| Construct | Meaning at this boundary |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pathway | Governed interaction structure; PylaiPathway uses it, Broker/Server reads do not manufacture it. |
| Praxis | Expected/permitted governing operational and assurance envelope; a Diadromí is not automatically a Praxis. |
| Journey | Canonical governed representation of observed/actual progression. Where applicable to the governed Journey it may record successful logical business interaction or technology/interoperability failure, without claiming unknown effects occurred or did not occur. |
| Audit | Significant auditable occurrence reported to Kleio; Journey may be a source but is not the audit boundary. |
| Assurance | Dokimasia may assess Journey against Praxis; evidence contribution does not make Pylai the assessor. |

Ponos remains the execution framework; Ergo the reusable conceptual activity
pattern/platform integration contract; Ergon the bounded fine-grained task-completion
component; Erga meaningful collections/assemblies/concurrent activity groupings.
Ergon remains Application Architecture behaviour with no discrete Information
Architecture representation merely by being an Ergon. Dokimasia's terminal
constraints remain intact. Pylai does not acquire these responsibilities through
participation in governed activity.
