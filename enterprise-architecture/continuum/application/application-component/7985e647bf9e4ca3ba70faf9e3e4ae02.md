### Accepted Information Unit Framework incorporation — 1.1.22

Mnemosyne interprets an [IU](../data-object/class/74ec609de1ae498fbf9d13e4ef8c6cbe.md)'s four InformationCoordinates Topic references (PersistenceGateway, GatewayEndpoint, InformationLocation, InformationOwner) to resolve provider/service and access context. They are logical names, independent of security and embedded network configuration. Coordinate presence/population follows the governing contract. Providers may use native physical storage/decomposition; Mnemosyne must reconstruct/deliver the IU's single governed representation and required identity/Metadata/Content consistency. Physical storage forms are not alternate IU representations. Authoritative durable state/version, meaningful outcomes and preservation remain Mnemosyne responsibilities. LifecycleContext supports Mneme–Mnemosyne handshaking; acceptance of work is not necessarily authoritative remote completion. GovernedPersistenceFramework/PersistenceService describe the accepted boundary roles without creating interfaces, deployments, universal state vocabulary, transaction machinery or transformation services. Detailed provider asymmetry, acknowledgement and recovery remain with their governing designs.

  [U11](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#u11), establishing authoritative durable state/version and the
  information/metadata required to sustain activity Context. Persistence conveys

  Mnemosyne does not become infrastructure management.
- **Information/outcomes:** durable representations/version/metadata, persistence

  uncertain effects remain explicit. Persistence logging is distinct from Kleio audit.
- **Search:** Mneme exposes Adhoc Search; Mnemosyne performs underlying authoritative

  Persistence-domain/shard/service/search-index capability metadata may be managed.
- **Research Metadata:** manages capture, persistence, management and search utilisation

  operational state, without acquiring management. No application-facing raw storage,
  AI derivation inside Mnemosyne, technology selection or workflow engine is established.
- **Standing:** U11 and explicit task decisions settle responsibilities; detailed
  oversight/transaction/compartment/outcome/search/Research Metadata contracts remain
  CDG-47/48, alongside historical structures CDG-12/13/40.
