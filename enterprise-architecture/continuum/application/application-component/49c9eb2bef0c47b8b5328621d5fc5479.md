# Ergon

## Architectural content

- **Role/responsibility:** a single well-defined software component encapsulating
  a bounded fine-grained task-completion activity set within the Ergo contract,
  executed/managed through Ponos. [U01/U02](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#u02), [L03/L19](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#l03).
- **Features:** specialised transformation/mapping, validation, identifier/reference
  checking, enrichment, routing, result processing, fan-out or registry-change
  behaviour. Reuses C05 common facilities; `ErgonBase` is historical contract
  support, not an architectural inheritance requirement.
- **Information:** acts upon or generates Pragma, payload/resource information,
  outputs, checkpoints and observations through governed contracts. It primarily
  acts on loaded Context values, may change working values and may generate new
  Content as **Pragma Output**, distinct from Context mutation. Bounded **Adhoc
  Resource Access / Adhoc Search** remains permitted for emerging needs. **No discrete
  Information Architecture representation or Ergon Information Resource exists
  merely because the component is an Ergon.** Activity is not information about it.
- **Output progression:** one or more execution outputs may yield multiple Pragma.
  The [Pathway-continuation role](../data-object/27ad143e7c784e378556af8a57c438f7.md)
  identifies information continuing the current Praxis; independently resulting
  Pragma remain available for governed use, without automatically advancing it.
- **Persistence/collaboration:** requests governed information use/change through
  Mneme; does not own Pragma management or durable state. Erga collects components;
  Ponos executes them; Petasos effects persistent handoff. Every Ergon exposes
  IngressEndpoint and EgressEndpoint C29; traversal does not expose internal logic.
  Praxis relationship detail remains open. The originating Ergon owns no persistence
  mechanics, shard/database transactions or generic conflict resolution; exceptional
  harmonisation is new governed work under U10.
- **Specialisations:** Behaviour C25, Dokimasia Ergon C26 and, through the latter,
  AI Assurance Ergon C27. They retain normal Ergo/Ergon facilities, constrained
  by the specialised role and Dokimasia's terminal rules where applicable.
- **Standing/exclusions:** bounded behaviour established for the candidate;
  competing atomic/pure/idempotent/branching claims remain CDG-05. An ordinary
  Ergon may intentionally invoke an AI service internally as an implementation
  choice without becoming AI Assurance Ergon or transferring authority.

Source: `docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/context-and-persistence-management.md` — ## Expected resource set and the Ergon information boundary. Standing: Candidate — adjudicated Domain05 Step-B baseline; final Solution Architecture unapproved.

**Praxis manages the definition of Context required by the Ergo framework** for
fulfilment of its Pathway. In R1.x/R2.x these requirements are concretely defined.
During development/definition, an **Ergo identifies the resource set it is most
likely to access**, within the applicable Context definitions. The set is
deliberately **not exhaustive**. The Ergo framework applies those definitions
and works with Mneme to efficiently load anticipated values before Ergon
Business Logic executes; preloading every conceivably accessible resource is
not required.

```text
Praxis Context definitions / Ergo likely resource declaration
    ↓
Expected / Likely Resource Set
    ↓
Mneme Context Load
    ↓
Context Loader
    ↓
Ergon Business Logic
    ↓
Context Unloader
    ↓
Mneme Context Save
```

| Boundary | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Mneme Context Load** | Locate/acquire, map, establish authoritative state/version basis, apply controls and compose values to materialise the managed Context. |
| **Ergo Context Loader** | Ordinarily exposes the **already-loaded Context** to Ergon Business Logic, without taking over Mneme resource acquisition. U14's [special Pylai ingress](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/pylai-external-interoperability.md#pylai-pathway-ingress) instead establishes initial Context/Task.input from Communication Information through established Mneme/persistence responsibilities; it performs no semantic mapping. |
| **Ergon Business Logic** | Perform bounded activity, primarily reading and acting upon values within its Context, with governed Adhoc acquisition where required. |
| **Context Unloader** | Bring resulting bounded processing back across the business-logic boundary, preserving resulting Context state and Pragma Outputs. It is **not a persistence mechanism**. |
| **Mneme Context Save** | Resume Mneme management of resulting Context state and the subsequent information consequences of Pragma Outputs; determine appropriate normal or exceptional handling. |

Context Load is the **preferred, performance-optimised common path**. Ergon
Business Logic SHOULD primarily use loaded values. It MAY use **Adhoc Resource
Access** and **Adhoc Search** where bounded activity requires runtime acquisition,
particularly for interaction with external systems, deep interrogation outside
reasonably known boundaries, or dynamically emerging information requirements.
Adhoc access may be materially more expensive than Context Load; it remains
legitimate. External interactions retain Pylai's established boundary.

Bounded Adhoc acquisition does not establish criteria-driven dynamic Context
determination. That direction, together with dynamically configured reusable/
generic Ergo, remains **deferred R3.x direction**; no mechanism or AI-driven
selection/configuration is introduced by [U18](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#u18).

The originating Ergon SHALL NOT be responsible for persistence mechanics,
database/shard selection, transactions, persistence tagging/indexing or generic
conflict resolution. No exact loader/unloader API, acquisition/batching strategy,
query language, locking/MVCC or Context concurrency algorithm is selected.

Source: `docs/markdown/04-information-architecture/patterns/search-results.md` — ## 3. External Information Search within an Ergo Activity. Standing: Adjudicated search boundary; exact source qualifications retained.

> **An external search performed by Harmonia is intentionally undertaken
> within an Ergo activity. Information returned by that search is acquired
> and persisted within the governed context of that activity and does not
> require a separate generic management-acceptance transition.**

For the architecture currently in scope, Harmonia does not randomly or
autonomously search external sources. The Ergo activity intentionally invokes
the search and provides the governed context in which the returned information
is obtained and persisted as activity information. Management responsibility
attaches within that activity's governed information context and applicable
Information Unit boundary; it is not a new acceptance decision merely because
the source is external. This establishes no universal Unit per returned item,
per external result or per activity.

```text
Ergo Activity
      |
      +-- intentionally invokes External Search
      |        |
      |        v
      |   Returned Information
      |        |
      +--------+
      |
      v
Governed Ergo Activity / Context
      |
      v
Persisted Activity Information
```

This is an intentional governed acquisition, not a rule that arbitrary
observation, storage or consumption establishes management. The generic
`Observed -> Accepted Into Management` transition is unnecessary for this
case. Originating authority remains source-qualified; acquisition confers no
authority over the underlying domain activity. The diagram expresses
information meaning, not proof that every invocation succeeds or persistence
has completed; AX-15 continues to preserve uncertain operational outcomes.

### 3.1 External Content and Semantic Opacity

Returned domain content may remain semantically opaque to Harmonia. Ergo
logic may inspect, apply, associate, transform, route, persist or otherwise act
upon it according to explicitly established behaviour. This does not imply
that Harmonia independently infers the content's domain meaning.

The established [observable-information and governed Ergo logic boundary](49c9eb2bef0c47b8b5328621d5fc5479.md)
continues to apply. No new execution allocation, intrinsic inference or
runtime-AI responsibility is established.

The Harmonia Platform is responsible for the governed execution boundary
around developer-defined Ergo business logic:

```text
Context Loading
      |
      v
Ergo Logic Process
      |
      v
Context Unloading
```

This is a conceptual boundary, not a new component allocation, interface,
runtime topology or definition of loading/unloading machinery. It does not
redesign Ergo/Ergon, Praxis, Pragma, Ponos or Digital Twin.

content sufficiently to perform its intended function. It may inspect

calculate results and produce outputs. Such intentional interpretation belongs
to that defined logic or invoked service; it does not imply intrinsic domain
comprehension by the Harmonia Platform. Platform governance and the applicable
work, information and domain-authority boundaries remain in force.

Source: `docs/markdown/04-information-architecture/guardrails/observable-information-and-domain-meaning.md` — ## 4. Runtime AI as an Optional Adjunct. Standing: Adjudicated observable-information boundary; runtime AI optional.

**Runtime AI is an adjunct execution capability intentionally invoked by
developer-defined Ergo business logic.** It is optional: Ergo execution may
use deterministic logic, rules/algorithms and governed information/services
without AI. Invocation does not make AI intrinsic to Harmonia or imply that
the Platform independently derives domain meaning.

Using an AI agent/service does not alter Ergo/Praxis/Digital Twin
responsibility, authority, governance or execution semantics. Architectural
responsibility remains with the governed Harmonia construct invoking the
capability. Invocation alone does not make the AI a Digital Twin, Business
Role, Service Guardian, governance authority, independent workflow authority
or owner of business responsibility, and transfers no architectural authority
to it. AI and assurance do not define Twin identity; the existing
[Strategy Digital Twin boundary](../../../../docs/markdown/02-strategy/strategic-views/logical-component-responsibilities.md#component-7-digital-twin-entity-centred-operational-coordination-construct)
remains controlling.

This general position does not allocate an AI-backed assurance activity or a
Dokimasia execution engine, or establish new AI governance, orchestration or
implementation. Assurance-specific uses and downstream execution allocation
are outside this bounded reconciliation; unestablished matters remain explicit
under AX-17.

### 4.1 Runtime AI and AI-Assisted Development

**AI-assisted development** uses AI tooling to design, document, analyse, test
or implement Harmonia. Using such tooling to build Harmonia does not make it a
participant in runtime business execution.

**Runtime AI** is an AI agent/service intentionally invoked by runtime Ergo
business logic to contribute to governed execution. The distinction concerns
the purpose of invocation, not the product name. Neither development-time use
nor runtime invocation establishes intrinsic Platform intelligence.
