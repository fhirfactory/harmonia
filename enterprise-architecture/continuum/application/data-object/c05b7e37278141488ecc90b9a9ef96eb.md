# Information Unit Metadata

## Architectural content

Information Unit Metadata is the existing logical DataObject for information-related
metadata of the same IU. **InformationMetadata** is its conceptual information
concern, peer to OperationalMetadata and conveyance metadata; it establishes no
mandatory wrapper, wholesale Class rename or shared physical owner. The IU's
identity and Mnemosyne-established authoritative persisted revision remain with
the IU. Existing native relationships and the enclosing IU Composition are preserved.

### Current information and operational allocation

| Concern | Current conceptual allocation and preserved boundary |
| --- | --- |
| InformationCoordinates | Unchanged four TopicID references: PersistenceGateway, GatewayEndpoint, InformationLocation, InformationOwner. They identify logical gateway capability, endpoint/parameter context, location/node and owner; Mnemosyne resolves them. They are not inline configuration, credentials or physical addresses. |
| InformationItinerary | Distinct information participation/path concern, separate from the four coordinates, information origin, provenance evidence, business Journey and an individual handoff. |
| SecurityContext | IU-side required action, compartment and other criteria; retained with information. Passport/identity permissions, PEP/PDP and Themis remain separate. A match grants no disclosure. |
| Information lifecycle | Governed preservation, immutable history, retention/hold, archive, supersession, RETIRE and authorised disposition remain information concerns. No universal native business-state enum is implied. |
| Relationships | Optional creator/updater-supplied references; existing local validation, no mandatory remote validation and no automatic reciprocal relationship obligations remain. |
| Mneme working/version/cache facts | Associated peer OperationalMetadata, relative to the IU or its manifestation as appropriate (OM-06/07). Mneme retains active-state and individual-IU concurrency responsibility. |
| Mneme–Mnemosyne handshake | Associated peer OperationalMetadata relative to the operation, including request/acceptance/commit/completion and operational times/correlations where governed (OM-08). The responsible contracts retain ownership. |

The semantic reallocation is **not a deletion of ManagementContext or LifecycleContext
from existing façade views**. Embedded, referenced and hybrid views remain valid.
Consumers must retain operational facts and guarantees required by accepted read,
write, Summary, search and cache contracts. The exact associated/reference/projection
representation is bounded detailed design, not an approved API-breaking migration.
InformationLifecycle describes governed information disposition; persistence-handshake
progress does not establish that disposition or multi-site durable completion.

### InformationItinerary and the parcel boundary

InformationItinerary describes components participating in actual or intended
information passage. SourceEntryComponent, CreationComponent, PreviousComponent,
NextComponent and TargetExitComponent are **conceptual candidates**, not a required
field catalogue. Historic participation and intended routing must be distinguishable;
their exact representation/update semantics remain detailed design. An itinerary
need not contain a fully known route and does not prove custody or replace Provenance.
Information origin remains separate from logical location and a parcel's source;
the exact overlapping origin/provenance association is unresolved detailed design.

Each relevant PetasosParcel carries **its own complete itinerary instance** for its
handoff. Complete means self-contained structure, not every value known/populated;
later handoffs must not retroactively mutate earlier parcels' historic context.
This is solely the approved common boundary. Parcel/Framework design, queue/retry/
routing/processing machinery and itinerary update algorithms remain deferred until
Mneme/Mnemosyne operational **0.5** build quality. No new Petasos element is allocated.

### Revision, manifestation and retention distinctions

| Value | Current meaning and conceptual home |
| --- | --- |
| Authoritative persisted IU revision | IU; established/returned by Mnemosyne. GovernedWriter may return a higher actual revision while preserving immutable history; authoritative reads cannot expose a pre-durable revision. |
| Mneme operational revision | Associated OperationalMetadata; cache coherence, stale-write/conflict and optimistic concurrency obligations unchanged. |
| Active Generation | Associated OperationalMetadata for a successfully established manifestation. Several generations may express one operational revision; no new IU, representation or version follows. |
| Content Structure Version | InformationRepresentation.ContentMetadata; version of its structure/model/profile. |
| Optional native Content instance version | Native Content where supplied; no synthetic native version, duplicate counter or promised equality with authoritative IU revision. |

The predecessor's three-version table did not exhaust Mnemosyne's authoritative
revision contract. This reconciliation makes that established role explicit; it
creates no additional implementation counter or mutation of native FHIR meta.versionId.
Active Generation is a manifestation identity, not another revision axis.

OperationalMetadata may be transient **or durable** according to the responsible
contract. Cache loss, eviction, parcel expiry or conceptual relocation must not
discard facts/work required for recovery, uncertainty reconciliation, audit/provenance,
or retention. Explicitly transient references can expire; they never become durable
truth. No common persistence transaction, central metadata service, schema, global
generation counter or mandatory UUID for every datum is prescribed.

Business/clinical event times remain in Content; structure versions in ContentMetadata;
working-cache and persistence-handshake observations are associated OperationalMetadata.
Security/session material is not automatically durable IU information. Kleio audit,
Dokimasia assurance/Provenance and operational logs retain their separate obligations.

### Neutral selectors

InformationUnitMask selects governed information characteristics;
OperationalMetadataMask selects facts about the declared operational subject/context;
PetasosParcelMask selects conveyance. They are separable neutral selectors, containing/
referencing common TopicMasks for Topic-valued criteria. Operational properties do not
silently re-enter an IU mask as intrinsic information. No field/operator/cardinality,
absence, observation/snapshot or event catalogue is invented. The optional operational
criterion and cross-mask conjunction are conceptual boundaries, not an implemented
subscription design. Themis Passport does not inherit from InformationUnitMask.

Retention holds, archival, immutable history, authorised disposition, recovery and
RETIRE versus physical destruction retain their distinct existing governance.

Optional **Relationships [0..*]** are creator/authorised-updater supplied IU-to-IU
or external-resource attributes/references. relatesTo, partOf and basedOn are
illustrative, not a mandatory vocabulary or a substitute for Provenance. Mneme
validates local reference acceptability under the applicable governed contract,
including suitable identity/authorised constraints; this is neither clinical-truth
validation nor target access permission. Mneme preserves remote references without
assuming responsibility for target existence, availability or correctness. There
is no automatic relationship inference, reciprocal maintenance, update propagation,
referent repair, distributed transaction or universal relationship database.

Provenance, AuditEvent, IU relationships and SecurityContext are distinct. Dokimasia
owns the established Provenance Management framework/lifecycle and independent
assurance; originating components use its existing Provenance Client Library.
Kleio retains AuditEvent management and authoritative audit capture. Mneme manages
active information; Mnemosyne establishes durable state. The IU carries appropriate
references/correlation without implementing evidence or lifecycle orchestration.
Request/session Passports/enforcement snapshots do not automatically become durable
IU Metadata or Content. Detailed field schemas, local-reference constraints and
provider acknowledgement/recovery vocabulary remain contract obligations.
