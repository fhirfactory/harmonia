# Pragma Topic

## Architectural content

**Mneme manages Pragma.** Context is the value-based working environment;
Pragma is the task/activity information envelope through which inputs and outputs
are expressed. They are related and distinct.

**Pragma realises [Metabolē](../../business/business-object/c34b4929f71946708d37dbe6c1da920a.md)**
for governed Harmonia information/activity progression. This is architectural
Realisation, not synonymy or inheritance from Diadromí. Mneme retains Pragma
management; the conceptual construct introduces no common manager, persistence
requirement or shared lifecycle with Pylai.Diadromí.

The conceptual
[ActivityContext](../../business/business-object/c34b4929f71946708d37dbe6c1da920a.md)
classifies the broad activity and is distinct from request action, this loaded
Context and Themis PEP-Context. Their detailed physical mapping remains deferred.

An Ergon may read existing Context values, act upon/change working Context
values, and generate **new Content**. New Content is expressed as **Pragma
Output**, rather than merely an incidental Context mutation:

```text
Existing Context values → Ergon → changed working Context values
Context + bounded business logic → Ergon → Pragma Output → new Content
                  Context Unloader returns both, distinctly
                                    ↓
                 Mneme manages subsequent information consequences
```

Changed Context values and newly generated Pragma Outputs SHALL remain
semantically distinguishable. A working change or produced output does not
itself establish authoritative durable state. Task/undertaking/outcome ownership
and output rules remain unchanged.

Ergon execution may produce one or more outputs, whose processing may result in
multiple Pragma. **Only a Pragma playing the Pathway-continuation role identifies
information for continuing the current Praxis along its Pathway**; other resulting
Pragma are independently available for governed Pub/Sub, another Praxis, other
processing or no further processing. Praxis defines continuation semantics;
the execution framework performs applicable output processing and routing,
without making Praxis an orchestrator/choreographer or transferring Mneme's
information management. See the [shared semantic view](27ad143e7c784e378556af8a57c438f7.md)
and [DesignNote](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/design-notes/DesignNote-Pragma-Outputs-and-Pathway-Continuation.md).
The continuation role creates no subtype. Whether a new Pragma is constructed,
an output Pragma is designated, or another governed representation is used remains
[explicitly deferred](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/gaps.md#continuation-representation). Exact Output
representation, routing mechanisms and Pragma/Information Unit mappings remain open.

Mneme may manage richer resulting Pragma relationships and provenance than the
originating Praxis's Journey represents. Continuation lineage supports meaningful
Journey progression without making Journey a complete provenance graph, execution
log, audit trail or queue history; [Journey's boundary](359b901df27f4b43b5a2ff14a8484e34.md)
and Energeia ownership remain intact.

Source standing: Bounded adjudicated application information meaning; Domain05 remains the accepted candidate Step-B baseline, not final Solution Architecture. Detailed representation is deferred; only this defined logical object is projected.

  common Pragma process-management, assurance, reporting and other management
  facilities. They introduce no new Domain04 Information Concepts here.
- **Persistence:** relevant Pragma operational information is an O&A classification
  candidate under U06. Legacy active-cache and FHIR Task/non-FHIR structure variants
  do not settle detailed history, representation or outcome contracts
  (CDG-02/03/12/13); the envelope is not authoritative merely by existing.
- **Continuation role:** [U18](27ad143e7c784e378556af8a57c438f7.md)
  distinguishes Pragma independently resulting from Ergon outputs from the Pragma
  used to continue the current Praxis along its Pathway. Continuation is a role,
  not a new subtype; construction versus output designation versus another governed
  representation remains deferred. Independent Pragma may have other governed
  use or no further processing. Mneme relationships/provenance may exceed the
  originating Journey's observed progression.
- **Collaboration/exclusions:** created/consumed/updated at ingress and execution,
  observed through presentation and outbound status handling. Does not execute
  business logic, own pools/consumer loops, define workflow blueprints or convey
  itself over wire transports.
- **Standing:** candidate carrier, not a complete Task/undertaking/outcome model
  or evidence repository. No universal state machine, automatic context persistence,
  TaskOutcome schema or ReportedTask meaning is approved here.

Source: `docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/pathways-journeys-and-handoff.md` — ## Ergon outputs and the Pathway-continuation role. Standing: Candidate — adjudicated Domain05 Step-B baseline; final Solution Architecture unapproved.

Ergon execution may produce one or more outputs; their processing may result in
multiple Pragma. **Not every resulting Pragma advances the current Pathway.**
The **Pathway-continuation Pragma** is the role identifying derived information
required to continue fulfilment of the current Praxis along its Pathway. Praxis
defines derivation semantics; the execution framework performs applicable output
processing and routing. Mneme retains management of all resulting Pragma and
Context; Petasos retains durable physical handoff where applicable.

Other resulting Pragma remain independently available governed artefacts for
Mneme Publish/Subscribe, another Praxis, other governed processing or no further
processing. Production alone establishes neither continuation of the current
Pathway nor a requirement to publish or consume each output.

**Continuation is a Pragma role, not a new subtype.** Whether continuation always
constructs a new Pragma, designates a resulting output Pragma, or uses another
governed representation is explicitly deferred. The adjudication and conceptual
branching example are in the [DesignNote](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/design-notes/DesignNote-Pragma-Outputs-and-Pathway-Continuation.md);
[CDG-53](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/gaps.md#continuation-representation) preserves the modelling decision.

**R1.x/R2.x Context requirements remain concretely defined.** Existing likely
resource declarations and bounded Adhoc access remain supported. R3.x may
introduce criteria-driven dynamic Context determination and dynamically configured
reusable/generic Ergo; that direction and its mechanisms remain deferred, with
no AI-driven selection/configuration introduced here.

Pragma **realises

Every Pragma contains or references a payload such as an Information Unit.
The working semantic shape relevant to Pattern evaluation is:

```text
Pragma
  Pragma Metadata
    origin / targetDestination / timestamps / processing status / ...
  Payload (contained or referenced)
    InformationUnit
      Container
        Topic / other metadata (including source)
      Content
        Topic / other metadata (including source)
```

This is semantic notation, not a final Java/API/storage schema. “Container”
denotes the Unit-level aspect in this application view; it neither reinstates
the retired **Information Container** concept nor replaces Domain04's
[Identity / Metadata / Content / Context partition](../../../../docs/markdown/04-information-architecture/metamodel/information-architecture-metamodel.md#61-conceptual-partition).
Its exact relationship to that partition remains a detailed representation
question; Identity and Management Context are not removed by this illustration.

**Topic is governed classification/metadata participating in Pattern evaluation.**
It may occur at more than one semantic level. Container Topic and Content Topic
need not mean the same thing. Topic is not synonymous with destination, queue
or channel; source, origin and targetDestination are not automatically synonyms.
Detailed metadata definitions and Container/Content Topic semantics remain
unresolved. Neither broker topic naming nor framework routing conventions
establish those definitions.

Source: `docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/design-notes/DesignNote-Pragma-Outputs-and-Pathway-Continuation.md` — ## 3. Outputs and continuation. Standing: Architecturally adjudicated U18; candidate scope and representation deferrals retained.

Execution of an Ergon may produce one or more outputs. Processing those outputs
may result in multiple Pragma. **Not every resulting Pragma implicitly progresses
the current Pathway.**

The **Pathway-continuation Pragma** represents the derived information required
to continue fulfilment of the current Praxis along its Pathway. Praxis defines
the semantics by which that continuation is derived. The execution framework
performs the applicable output processing and routing using those semantics.

Conceptually:

```text
Pragma X
    |
    v
  Ergo A
    |
    v
Pragma X.output
    |
    v
Process Pragma Output
    |
    +----> Independent Pragma P1 ---> Mneme Pub/Sub / other governed use
    |
    +----> Independent Pragma P2 ---> another Praxis / other consumer
    |
    +----> Pathway-continuation Pragma X'
                              |
                              v
                            Route
                              |
                              v
                            Ergo B
```

This is a conceptual distinction, not a processing algorithm, identity rule,
mandatory fan-out cardinality or physical pipeline. Other resulting Pragma are
independently available governed artefacts: they may participate in Mneme
Publish/Subscribe, another Praxis, other governed Harmonia processing, or no
further processing. Availability does not imply publication, selection, handoff
or execution, and does not bypass applicable governance.
