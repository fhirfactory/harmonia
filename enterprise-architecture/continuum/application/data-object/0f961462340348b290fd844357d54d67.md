# Praxis

## Architectural content

- **Role/ownership:** higher-order operational construct defining expected/permitted
  context and the operational/assurance envelope. **Energeia C39 owns Praxis**
  and provides **Praxis Management Services F11** under [U06](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/sources.md#u06).
  It is not another execution engine or necessarily an independent component.
- **Fulfilment semantics:** Pathway defines the conceptual Ergo sequence; Praxis
  defines how it is fulfilled: applicable Context definitions required by the
  Ergo framework, segment criteria/outcomes, continuation derivation semantics
  and other Praxis-level housekeeping. Ponos/Ergo performs output processing and
  routing. Context requirements are concrete in R1.x/R2.x; criteria-driven dynamic
  Context and dynamically configured reusable/generic Ergo remain deferred R3.x
  direction, without an AI-driven selection/configuration mechanism.
- **Information/relationships:** candidate interface configuration, Topic exclusions
  and potentially inclusions, operational constraints/configuration and assurance
  rules. **Aggregation: Pathway [0..1]**; working **Aggregation: Journey** with
  cardinalities unresolved. No Composition or shared lifecycle is inferred.
  Praxis may exist without a Pathway for Publish/Subscribe activity.
  The specialised [Digital Twin Behaviour pattern](../application-component/a26f06d66bec4981b266e202c46a9afb.md) uses a Pathway with
  exactly one Ergo; it retains full Praxis semantics and does not change the
  general Aggregation cardinality.
- **Management/persistence:** Mneme manages governed representations; Mnemosyne
  **C&G** provides durable persistence responsibility. Ponos consumes Praxis for
  execution and Dokimasia uses it as the primary normative assurance basis;
  neither use nor persistence transfers application ownership.
- **Retained detail:** schema, lifecycle/versioning, activation/deactivation,
  replacement/retirement and in-flight effects remain CDG-01/06. Historical
  TaskSequence/PraxisDefinition/PraxisImplementation and A04 actual-assurance-
  activity descriptions do not establish equivalence or a definition-to-instance
  model. U06 supersedes the earlier wholly unresolved broader-Praxis standing.
- **Logical boundary:** [Praxis.Horos C46](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/component-register.md#c46), with IngressHoros/EgressHoros,
  names the Praxis boundary. EgressHoros hands interaction state/outcome back
  before Ergo.ContextUnloader completes; it is not a technical endpoint.
- **Exclusions:** Praxis is not Pathway, Route, a sequence of Ergo/Ergon, an
  orchestrator, choreographer, runtime engine or Pub/Sub graph. Mneme semantic
  selection, Petasos concrete persistent handoff and Ponos execution remain distinct.
  No detailed Praxis schema is invented.

Source: `docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/pathways-journeys-and-handoff.md` — ## Praxis: operational context and assurance basis. Standing: Candidate — adjudicated Domain05 Step-B baseline; final Solution Architecture unapproved.

**Praxis is the higher-order operational construct establishing the operational
and assurance envelope within which Harmonia activity occurs.** The conversational
description “super Pathway/Journey” identifies its higher-order context; it
establishes neither a supertype nor an execution engine. Praxis defines expected
or permitted operational context, including candidate externally visible interface
information, overall Topic exclusions and potentially inclusions, operational
configuration/constraints and assurance rules/expectations.

**Pathway defines the conceptual Ergo sequence; Praxis defines how that Pathway
is fulfilled.** Praxis manages the definition of Context required by the Ergo
framework, expected criteria/outcomes for Pathway segments, output-to-next-input
continuation semantics and other Praxis-level fulfilment housekeeping. Ponos/Ergo
performs governed execution, output processing and routing using those semantics;
**Praxis is neither an orchestrator nor a choreographer**.

The [U19 Digital Twin Behaviour pattern](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/design-notes/DesignNote-Digital-Twin-Behaviour-and-Execution-Semantics.md)
is a genuine one-Ergo Praxis: Digital Twin → Behaviour [1..*] → Praxis →
Pathway → Ergo [exactly 1]. Required Context, criteria/outcomes, fulfilment and
housekeeping still apply. This specialised realisation does not alter the general
Praxis → Pathway Aggregation [0..1]. Energeia and Ponos are peer application
components; Praxis's semantic/operational envelope does not structurally contain
Ponos. Behaviour sequencing is not Pathway orchestration.

**Energeia owns the Praxis application responsibility** and SHALL provide the
candidate **Praxis Management Services** responsibility set:

- definition and registration;
- discovery and retrieval;
- operational configuration and externally visible interface configuration;
- Topic inclusion/exclusion policy;
- Pathway relationship/aggregation management;
- applicable Context definitions, segment criteria/outcomes and continuation semantics;
- assurance-rule association and other Praxis-wide operational constraints.

These responsibilities are not yet decomposed into separate Application Features,
Application Services, internal constructs or subordinate Software Components.
Topic inclusion's necessity, the interface model and assurance-rule attachment
contract remain unresolved. Managing interface configuration does not transfer
Pylai's external contract/publication responsibility to Energeia; Praxis Topic
policy does not transfer Mneme's Publishing/Subscription/Pattern selection.

| Architectural relationship | Established meaning and limit |
| :--- | :--- |
| Energeia → Praxis | Application ownership/management through Praxis Management Services. Mneme manages its governed information; Ponos uses it for execution; Dokimasia uses it as assurance basis. |
| Praxis → Pathway | **Aggregation [0..1]**. Praxis may exist without a Pathway because activity may proceed through Publish/Subscribe. |
| Praxis → Journey | Working **Aggregation** of relevant observed progression; exact cardinalities and association contracts remain unresolved. No Composition or shared lifecycle is inferred. |
| Journey → contributing information/evidence | Semantic **Aggregation/reference** without transfer of originating ownership or indiscriminate duplication. |

Praxis is not equivalent to Pathway, Route, a sequence of Ergo/Ergon, a runtime
engine or a Pub/Sub graph. Historical blueprint/composition/execution descriptions
are retained provenance, not competing current definitions. Detailed Praxis
representation and lifecycle remain [CDG-01/06](../../../../docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/gaps.md): versioning,
activation/deactivation, replacement, retirement and treatment of in-flight
activity following change are deliberately unestablished.

Source: `docs/markdown/05-information-systems-architecture/design-notes/DesignNote-Pragma-Outputs-and-Pathway-Continuation.md` — ## 2. Pathway and Praxis. Standing: Architecturally adjudicated U18; candidate scope and representation deferrals retained.

**A Pathway defines the conceptual sequence of Ergo through which execution
progresses.** Established PathwayEndpoints, PathwaySegments and Routes express
the controlled interactions available along that sequence; their existing
meanings remain intact.

**A Praxis defines how that Pathway is to be fulfilled**, including:

- the Context definitions applicable to execution;
- expected criteria/outcomes associated with Pathway segments;
- the semantics required to process one segment's result into the input required
  by the next; and
- other Praxis-level housekeeping required for fulfilment.

Praxis manages the definition of Context required by the Ergo framework. Mneme
manages Context materialisation and use; the framework applies the applicable
definitions. The existing non-exhaustive likely resource set identified by an
Ergo and supported bounded Adhoc access remain compatible with that division.

This clarifies fulfilment semantics within Praxis's established operational and
assurance envelope. It preserves Praxis Aggregation of Pathway [0..1], including
the established possibility of Pub/Sub activity without a predefined Pathway.
It does not make Praxis an orchestrator, choreographer or execution engine.
