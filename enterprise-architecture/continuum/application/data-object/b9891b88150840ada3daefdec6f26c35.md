# InformationRepresentation

## Architectural content

An InformationRepresentation is the singular governed presentation of the Content
of one InformationUnit. Each IU contains **exactly one InformationRepresentation
[1..1]**. This is a logical structured DataObject; it establishes no implementation
Class, independently governed nested IU or additional information identity.

| Constituent concern | Accepted meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ContainerMetadata | Native container, envelope, assembly and carriage semantics: relevant source/destination, author/recipient, assembly and interaction context. Native envelope semantics are preserved where governed; they are not invariably disposable transport state or IU Metadata. |
| ContentMetadata | Describes the represented Content independently of its values. Includes a Topic describing Content Structure and a Content Structure Version. Other characteristics, where governed, may include type/profile/model, concrete format/encoding, language, size, hash/digest and references. |
| Content | The actual native information, structured/textual/binary/composite/opaque, inline or referenced, FHIR or non-FHIR. Native instance version, if supported (for example FHIR meta.versionId), remains inside Content; none is fabricated for an unversioned payload. |

**Unit Structure ≠ Content Structure ≠ Content Format.** ContentMetadata.Version
means Content Structure Version, not Mneme operational Version or native payload
instance version. Container Metadata, Content Metadata and IU Metadata remain
distinguishable. These constituents are semantic structure, not additional Classes,
Topics, schemas or a mandatory list of populated fields.

The IU maintains no alternate-representation collection, otherRepresentations(),
reverse-transformation rules or synchronisation dependencies. A transforming
consumer creates a **new IU with its own single representation** when the result
is to be governed, with appropriate Provenance and any expressly supplied optional
relationships. Transformation fidelity and any attempted reverse update remain
that consumer's business logic; source updates remain governed. Lossy or irreversible
transformations confer no automatic backlink or source-change obligation.

Mnemosyne and its PersistenceServices may decompose or reference physical storage
and use provider-native forms. Retrieval must faithfully reconstruct/deliver the
governed IU contract with required identity, Metadata/Content integrity and accepted
state consistency. Provider-native storage is not another governed representation.
Native Content integrity/byte fidelity and preservation obligations retain scope;
large referenced binaries need not occupy one row, file or physical write.

Content-aware and Content-neutral services/providers may participate under their
own governed contracts without redefining the IU. Content-aware integration may
inspect/transform relevant ContainerMetadata under its owning Feature; this does
not transfer integration business logic to Mnemosyne or require Content-neutral
providers to interpret native payload meaning.
