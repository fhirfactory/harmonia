\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{3.0cm} P{3.8cm} P{3.8cm} Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Dimension} & \textbf{Used / Relied Upon} & \textbf{Avoided / Excluded} & \textbf{Architectural Rationale} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Synchronization} & Synchronous replication (\nolinkurl{REPL_SYNC}) across all 17 caches & Asynchronous replication (\nolinkurl{REPL_ASYNC}) without ack & Synchronous caching guarantees read-your-writes consistency across pods. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Transport Protocol} & Hot Rod Binary Protocol (port 11222) & REST, Memcached, WebSocket protocols & Hot Rod binary RPC minimizes serialization latency and payload overhead. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Discovery} & JGroups TCP discovery (port 7800) over headless service & JGroups UDP multicast discovery & UDP multicast is unreliable or prohibited in standard Kubernetes container networks. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Query \& Indexing} & Key-based lookup over text/plain payloads & Lucene / Hibernate Search on cache entries & Search indexing is delegated to PostgreSQL; avoids memory exhaustion. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Transactions} & Non-transactional caches with write-behind SPI & 2-Phase Commit (2PC) / XA distributed transactions & Eliminates 2PC distributed locking overhead; relies on idempotent workflows. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Cross-Site HA} & Single-cluster multi-pod grid & Cross-site replication (x-site across data centers) & Local HA handles node recovery; cross-site deferred to future phases. \\
\bottomrule

\subsection{PostgreSQL 16 Relational Persistence}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{3.0cm} P{3.8cm} P{3.8cm} Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Dimension} & \textbf{Used / Relied Upon} & \textbf{Avoided / Excluded} & \textbf{Architectural Rationale} \\
\midrule
\textbf{DB Isolation} & Strictly separated clinical, operations, and Synapse DBs & Single unified monolithic database schema & Prevents operational telemetry queries from impacting clinical ACID transaction throughput. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Persistence Schema} & Relational tables with immutable composite primary keys & In-place row updates without version tracking & Guarantees complete historical auditability and temporal reproducibility of FHIR records. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Indexing Strategy} & Targeted composite B-Tree indexes on lookup and timestamps & Full-database automated or unchecked index sprawl & Balances fast sub-millisecond retrieval with high-throughput write performance. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Logic Placement} & Schema definitions, constraints, and standard SQL queries & Database triggers, PL/pgSQL stored procedures, DB rules & Business logic remains in testable Java/Spring services (Themis, Energeia, Mnemosyne). \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Replication} & Independent node instances partitioned by domain & Native PostgreSQL streaming replication / Patroni & Simplifies base container orchestration; HA provided by application-tier redundancy and K8s. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
