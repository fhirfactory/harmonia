\subsection*{2. Client Administration}
\phantomsection\label{rad:em:13}

Client Administration governs the identity, demographic context, relationship associations, and privacy directives of persons receiving care.

\phantomsection\label{rad:em:15}
\subsubsection*{2.1 Person Identity}
\phantomsection\label{rad:em:16}

\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Owning Capability}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Person Identity}} (under \texttt{\seqsplit{Client Administration}})



\item 
\textbf{Key Architectural Semantics}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Identifier Resolution}: Asks for authoritative detail and verification regarding a specific person identifier within a single domain namespace.



\item 
\textbf{Identifier Correlation}: Ingests and maintains cross-authority associations between identifier namespaces where the governing relationship is established by authoritative information.



\item 
\textbf{Identity Alias}: Contextual, legal, or alternative identity expressions (e.g., preferred names, maiden names, emergency aliases) by which a person is known. It is conceptually distinct from cross-domain correlation.



\item 
\textbf{R1.x/\allowbreak{}R2.x authority boundary}: Harmonia ingests and maintains authoritative identifiers, aliases and identity relationships, processes externally authoritative corrections/\allowbreak{}merge outcomes and propagates managed-state consequences. It does not originate probabilistic matching, golden-record determination, authoritative master-person selection or person-merge decisions. \href{https://github.com/fhirfactory/harmonia/blob/98410db60155198a75f74c79d98060070209faa1/docs/markdown/01-motivation/requirements-constraints/foundational-requirements.md\#req-fnd-003-subject-referential-integrity}{REQ-FND-003} and the \hyperref[rad:enabling:59]{Strategy scope statement} govern this boundary.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Functions \& Exposed Services}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Feature: Identifier Resolution}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Resolve Person Identifier}} — Validates and retrieves the authoritative demographic record bound to a specific identifier.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Person Identifier Resolution}} — Exposed to Service Administration and Service Delivery to disambiguate client identities.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Cross-Authority Identifier Correlation}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Correlate Person Identifiers}} — Correlates local identifiers (e.g., Hospital MRN, National IHI, Pathology ID) using relationships established by authoritative information, retaining their source and namespace.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Person Identifier Correlation}} — Exposed across the HIE to establish patient cross-reference mappings.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Identity Alias Association}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Person Identity Aliases}} — Records and qualifies alias names, titles, and emergency aliases for a governed person identity.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \emph{(Internal Function — accessed via Person Identifier Resolution)}.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Governed Identity Correction}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Apply Governed Person Identity Correction}} — Processes externally authoritative corrections and merge/\allowbreak{}unlink outcomes, updates governed identity associations and propagates resulting managed-state consequences with audit history. Harmonia does not adjudicate the originating person-merge decision.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Person Identity Correction}} — Exposed to authorized HIM/\allowbreak{}Medical Records administrators.



\item 
\emph{Governed Process}: \textbf{Governed Person Identity Correction Process}.



\end{itemize}


\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Information Responsibility}: Person Identity Record, Identifier Namespace Bindings, Cross-Authority Correlation Graph, Identity Aliases, Identity Merge/\allowbreak{}Split Audit Log.



\end{itemize}

\subsection*{3. Provider Administration}
\phantomsection\label{rad:em:84}

Provider Administration governs individual healthcare practitioners, their professional qualifications, clinical roles, electronic communication endpoints, and organizational affiliations.

\phantomsection\label{rad:em:86}

\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Owning Capability}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Provider Administration}}



\item 
\textbf{Key Architectural Semantics}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Practitioner ≠ Service Provider}: A \emph{Practitioner} is an individual professional. A \emph{Service Provider} is an organisation or facility offering health services. A Practitioner may act in the \emph{Service Provider} role in private practice.



\item 
\textbf{Role and privilege}: Practitioner Role Resolution supplies role/\allowbreak{}capacity information; it does not establish Clinical Privilege or Operational Privilege. Clinical Privilege Verification retains its clinical scope. An activity may require both distinct privilege determinations; neither follows from Role information alone.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Functions \& Exposed Services}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Feature: National Practitioner Verification}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Verify Practitioner Registration}} — Validates national registration, specialty endorsements, and disciplinary sanctions against regulatory registries (e.g., AHPRA).



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Practitioner Verification Service}} — Discloses verified registration and status to credentialing and clinical systems.



\item 
\emph{Governed Process}: \textbf{Practitioner Verification Process}.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Practitioner Profile Governance}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Govern Practitioner Profile}} — Maintains official names, professional identifiers (e.g., HPI-I, Prescriber Number), and clinical qualifications.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Practitioner Directory Query}} — Provides provider directory lookups across the healthcare network.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Role \& Specialty Modeling}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Practitioner Roles}} — Assigns and maintains clinical roles (e.g., Specialist Physician, Registrar, General Practitioner) fulfilled by a practitioner.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Practitioner Role Resolution}} — Resolves the specific clinical capacities of a practitioner.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Electronic Endpoint Binding}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Bind Practitioner Electronic Endpoints}} — Associates secure messaging addresses, direct communication endpoints, and notification preferences to practitioner roles.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Practitioner Endpoint Lookup}} — Provides secure message routing destinations for clinical communication.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Practitioner-Organisation Affiliation}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Practitioner Affiliations}} — Tracks employment, admitting rights, and consulting appointments at specific healthcare facilities.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Practitioner Affiliation Resolution}} — Discloses organizational ties and practice locations.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Clinical Privileging Record}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Clinical Privileges}} — Records approved clinical scopes of practice, procedural authorizations, and prescribing privileges.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Clinical Privilege Verification}} — Verifies procedural and prescribing authority for clinical ordering systems.



\end{itemize}


\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Information Responsibility}: Practitioner Master Record, Professional Registration Status, Practitioner Role Bindings, Electronic Communication Endpoints, Scope of Practice Privileges.



\end{itemize}

\subsection*{5. Location Administration}
\phantomsection\label{rad:em:138}

Location Administration defines the physical, functional, and geospatial structure of healthcare environments, including sites, buildings, floors, wards, rooms, bays, and bed care-places.

\phantomsection\label{rad:em:140}

\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Owning Capability}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Location Administration}}



\item 
\textbf{Key Architectural Semantics}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Definition vs. Operational State}: \emph{Location Administration} defines what a physical location or care-place \textbf{IS} (its static attributes, type, capacity, hierarchy). \emph{Health Service Operations} owns what is \textbf{operationally happening} to that care-place (occupancy, cleaning status, isolation locks, turnover).



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Functions \& Exposed Services}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Feature: Location Geospatial \& Address Resolution}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Healthcare Location}} — Records physical sites, campuses, outpatient clinics, and community health centres.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Location Directory Query}} — Exposes location profiles and geospatial addresses.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Physical Hierarchy Modeling}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Physical Location Hierarchy}} — Models the nested structural containment tree: \texttt{\seqsplit{Campus}} → \texttt{\seqsplit{Building}} → \texttt{\seqsplit{Floor}} → \texttt{\seqsplit{Ward/Suite}} → \texttt{\seqsplit{Room/Bay}} → \texttt{\seqsplit{Bed Care-Place}}.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Location Hierarchy Resolution}} — Discloses physical containment and navigation paths for clinical facilities.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Atomic Care-Place Modeling}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Care-Place Definition}} — Defines static bed bays, treatment chairs, surgical tables, and emergency resuscitation cubicles, including equipment capabilities and oxygen/\allowbreak{}telemetry infrastructure.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Care-Place Specification Lookup}} — Discloses care-place physical capabilities to Bed \& Care-Place Management.



\end{itemize}


\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Information Responsibility}: Location Master Registry, Physical Facility Hierarchy Tree, Care-Place Structural Definitions.



\end{itemize}

\subsection*{6. Health Service Administration}
\phantomsection\label{rad:em:159}

Health Service Administration governs the catalogue of clinical services offered across the healthcare network and resolves the critical multi-dimensional mapping between Services, Providers, Locations, and Practitioners.

\phantomsection\label{rad:em:161}

\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Owning Capability}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Health Service Administration}}



\item 
\textbf{Key Architectural Semantics}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Service /\allowbreak{} Location /\allowbreak{} Provider Map}: This capability models the multi-way relationship: Healthcare Service ↔ Service Provider ↔ Healthcare Location ↔ Practitioner Role



\item 
\textbf{Ownership Invariant}: The mapping capability owns the \textbf{relationships and association rules}. It does \textbf{not} acquire ownership of the Service, Provider, Location, or Practitioner entities being mapped.



\item 
\textbf{Consumer Resolution Scenarios}: Enables clinical and administrative consumers to unambiguously resolve:


\begin{enumerate}

\item 
\emph{Who provides Service X?}



\item 
\emph{Where does Provider Y provide Service X?}



\item 
\emph{What Services are delivered at Location Z?}



\item 
\emph{Where can Service X be obtained?}



\item 
\emph{Which Practitioner Roles participate in Service X?}



\end{enumerate}


\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Functions \& Exposed Services}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\textbf{Feature: Service Catalogue Publishing}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Healthcare Service Definition}} — Defines clinical service offerings (e.g., Cardiology Outpatient Clinic, MRI Imaging, Acute Stroke Care), specialty classifications, and clinical scope.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Healthcare Service Directory Query}} — Exposes published clinical service offerings.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Service-Location-Provider Binding}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Service / Location / Provider Map}} — Binds health service definitions to delivering organisations, physical clinic locations, and participating practitioner roles.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Service Provision Resolution}} — Exposes multi-dimensional resolution queries answering who, where, and when healthcare services are delivered.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Operating Schedule Specification}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Service Availability}} — Governs regular operating hours, emergency intake availability, and seasonal clinic schedules.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Service Availability Query}} — Discloses operational intake hours and open status.



\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Feature: Eligibility \& Catchment Specification}:


\begin{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Function}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Maintain Service Eligibility Rules}} — Configures catchment criteria, age thresholds, referral requirements, and clinical indications for service access.



\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Service Eligibility Evaluation}} — Evaluates referral eligibility for intake coordinators.



\end{itemize}


\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Information Responsibility}: Healthcare Service Catalogue, Service /\allowbreak{} Location /\allowbreak{} Provider Mapping Matrix, Service Availability Schedules, Service Eligibility Rule Sets.



\end{itemize}

\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Device Association Query}} — Discloses active device-to-patient and device-to-location bindings.

\item 
\textbf{Feature: Device Endpoint Registration}:

\item 
\emph{Exposed Service}: \texttt{\seqsplit{Device Endpoint Resolution}} — Exposes network addresses for telemetry routing.



\end{itemize}


\end{itemize}


\item 
\textbf{Information Responsibility}: Medical Device Type Registry, Physical Device Instance Directory, Device Association Ledger, Device Communication Endpoint Map.



\end{itemize}
