## Material decisions to review

**HIGH — BEC decomposition.** Seven cohesive proposed roots replace dependence on
the five contextual views. Entity administration remains CT2 with distinct CT3
identity/relationship/definition abilities. Most care-administration, care-context
and operational responsibilities are themselves CT3s beneath newly proposed CT2
frames. This avoids giving every existing heading a one-child intermediate tier.

**HIGH — Integrated Enterprise hierarchy.** Three proposed roots organise the
fourteen Canonical responsibilities as CT2s. Selected EC-04, EC-06, EC-07 and EC-10
responsibilities are decomposed where materially different reusable abilities
exist. Other ECs deliberately stop at CT2. Information Authority Qualification is
a new Proposed CT2; it represents credibility standing, not clinical truth or
independent service assurance.

**HIGH — Collective service readiness.** The existing Candidate readiness concern
receives Proposed BC CT3 placement beneath a new preparedness CT2 in Care Access
& Coordination. A distinct Proposed BEC assembles dependency evidence. The
alternative placement in Health Service Direction & Stewardship remains plausible;
both the business placement and information-versus-decision boundary need review.

**HIGH — Responsibility does not follow use.** Clinical facts, professional
privilege, source roster/booking authority, public-health decisions, research
approval and governance approval remain with their mandated owners. Audit capture,
lineage, operational recovery and assurance conclusions retain different
responsibilities. No application component is allocated by these proposals.

**HIGH — Clinical contexts overlap.** The proposed longitudinal/community,
facility/episodic and specialist CT2 frames organise integration purposes. They are
not exclusive classes of care. Acute capture, inpatient clinical progression and
screening recall retain their canonical composition-level sufficiency decisions;
no specialised clinical capture engines are inferred.

**MEDIUM — Feature completeness.** Additional Features frame specific missing
behaviour: preliminary/corrected diagnostic information, order outcomes, historical
reconstruction, definition/binding/profile governance, local capacity, governed
reference-content revision, binary content, entity-relative activity, administrative
recovery and independent assurance. Existing directly owned Functions remain
Capability-owned; proposed realisation links do not silently change accountability.

**MEDIUM — Name and qualification boundaries.** The current display name Health
Information Exchange Enablement is Proposed for the Canonical BEC. Its stable ID,
label, established name and governed definition remain unchanged. This resolves the
current-model name collision without claiming that RADS has accepted the rename.
Clinical Qualification Management and physical badge/access Credential Management
remain different capabilities; the inherited Operational Credential Check Feature
retains its clinical qualification definition despite its potentially confusing name.

**MEDIUM — Prior candidate reconciliation.** The old Enterprise coexistence branch
is removed. Generic Context Establishment, State Monitoring, Exception Management
and retrieval patterns are retained as scoped concerns/reuse evidence where they
describe no additional Feature outcome. Useful specific deltas receive explicit
proposals; no candidate table adjacency becomes an established behavioural edge.

**LOW — Structural conventions.** New semantic local codes are category-qualified.
The proposed EC hierarchy uses the existing typed structural grammar as an explicit
Step 2 extension. Historical EC-01 through EC-14 structural tokens resolve directly
to their current identities; they are not reallocated to another element.

## Preserved tensions and limitations

The v0.1 source appendix deliberately still says some tiers and derivations are
unresolved. Those are truthful baseline statements; this publication proposes
current answers without changing that history. Clinical device maintenance remains
Candidate despite proposed membership beside the canonical seven-child set.
Sharing-programme meaning is weakly evidenced and receives a low-confidence
Proposed elaboration rather than a fabricated canonical definition. The governance
publication BEC is a narrow proposal for BC-17 support, whose RAD relevance and
enablement questions remain unadjudicated.

The Strategy assurance derivation retains earlier statements that downstream
Functions/Services/Processes remain unestablished, while the current Domain03
assurance catalogue explicitly establishes them. Both sources are preserved; the
five established Functions and existing exposure/progression relationships form
the semantic anchor here. Similarly, the bounded B2.1 account records a later
mapping state than some Domain05 overview paragraphs. No programme completion or
authority precedence is inferred merely from those inconsistent status summaries.

This is a complete current proposal within Harmonia's bounded capability scope,
not an exhaustive hospital enterprise architecture. Externally provided
authentication, roster/booking machinery, clinical decision-making and uncommitted
R3 master-person reconciliation remain boundary context. Detailed assurance
information models, universal lifecycle states, outcome taxonomies, binary custody
technology and physical deployment remain downstream questions.
