# Architectural Axioms — Motivation Orientation

## Overview

This is a non-authoritative orientation and navigation page. The
[canonical governance register](../../governance/architectural-axioms.md)
is the single maintained authoritative register for AX-01 through AX-18,
governing the whole Harmonia architecture.

Motivation relates the axioms to drivers, goals, outcomes and requirements.
This page maintains no normative axiom definitions or separate classification.
AX-12 remains a current cross-domain axiom; the
[earlier transfer claim is superseded](reclassified-principles.md).
AX-18 is a new human-approved decision established on 2026-10-09.
