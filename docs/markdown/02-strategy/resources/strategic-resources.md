# Strategic Resources Catalogue & Candidate Adjudication

## Overview

This catalogue establishes Harmonia's minimal, enduring **Strategic Resources**—the foundational external specifications, directory/identifier frameworks, and clinical vocabularies upon which Harmonia depends to realise its capabilities and courses of action.

Every asset considered has been evaluated against the **Strategic Significance Test**:
> *"If this asset disappeared or became unavailable, would Harmonia's strategic ability to realise its intended capabilities or Courses of Action materially change?"*

This catalogue also provides formal disposition records for candidate assets relegated to other architecture domains.

## 2. Admitted Strategic Resources

### SR-01: Healthcare Interoperability Standards and Specifications

#### Strategic Definition
**Healthcare Interoperability Standards and Specifications** comprise the external, open, normative specifications governing healthcare data structures, clinical resource models, exchange semantics, and conformance criteria across enterprise and regional boundaries.

#### Enduring Strategic Role
Harmonia is architected to facilitate vendor-neutral health data exchange across acute, primary, community, and diagnostic healthcare systems. This interoperability cannot be accomplished using proprietary vendor protocols. Harmonia depends directly on consensus-based, open standards to establish:
- Standardised structural and semantic representations for clinical entities (encounters, observations, conditions, medications, diagnostic reports).
- Conformance validation frameworks to assert that emitted and ingested payloads comply with normative specifications.
- Standards-conformant external interaction contracts across the Harmonia boundary.

#### Current Strategic Instance
- **HL7® FHIR® Release 5 Core & Australian Profile Specifications**: The principal contemporary instance of this resource is HL7 FHIR Release 5, alongside Australian jurisdictional profile specifications (AU Base and AU Core).
- **Technology Independence**: The strategic resource is the **specification and standard itself**, not any specific implementation framework or runtime library (such as HAPI FHIR). The strategic dependency endures regardless of which runtime software serialises or deserialises the specifications.

#### Traceability
- **Architectural Axioms**: `AX-02` (Boundary Membrane), `AX-03` (Native Standards), `AX-13` (Explicit Management Boundary).
- **Enterprise Capabilities**: `EC-08` (Interoperability & Exchange), `EC-13` (Semantic Governance & Conformance).
- **Courses of Action**: `COA-01` (Boundary Membrane Sovereignty).

### SR-02: Australian National Healthcare Directory & Identifier Specifications

#### Strategic Definition
**Australian National Healthcare Directory & Identifier Specifications** comprise the jurisdictional normative specifications, identifier schemes, and federation protocols defining how healthcare providers, organisations, and service delivery endpoints are identified, addressed, and verified across Australian digital health.

#### Enduring Strategic Role & Distinct Facets
Harmonia depends upon external national specifications to participate in federated healthcare discovery and directed clinical message routing. To maintain architectural clarity, Harmonia distinguishes the complementary facets of this resource:
1. **National Healthcare Directory & Locator Specifications**:
   - Normative specifications governing federated directory lookup, provider service discovery, and electronic service delivery endpoint resolution (e.g., Australian National Directory specifications, Endpoint Directory Service, and Location Directory Service).
   - Enables Harmonia to resolve destination endpoints for clinical communications without relying on brittle point-to-point configurations.
2. **Healthcare Identifier (HI) Ecosystem Specifications**:
   - Normative identifier schemes, metadata structures, and verification service specifications for Australian healthcare entities:
     - **HPI-I**: Healthcare Provider Identifier - Individual (practitioners).
     - **HPI-O**: Healthcare Provider Identifier - Organisation (health service organisations).
     - **IHI**: Individual Healthcare Identifier (patients / healthcare recipients).
   - Establishes jurisdictional identity anchors that Harmonia must correlate across diverse regional identifiers (e.g., hospital MRNs, practice local IDs).

#### Traceability
- **Architectural Axioms**: `AX-01` (Health-Information Centric), `AX-06` (Explicit Authority), `AX-14` (Preserve Distinctions).
- **Enterprise Capabilities**: `EC-01` (Managed Entity & Relationship), `EC-08` (Interoperability & Exchange).
- **Courses of Action**: `COA-01` (Boundary Membrane Sovereignty), `COA-05` (Entity-Centred Operational Coordination).

### SR-03: National Clinical Terminology Assets (SNOMED CT-AU / AMT)

#### Strategic Definition
**National Clinical Terminology Assets** comprise the authoritative, governed clinical vocabularies, concept identifiers, descriptions, and semantic relationships used to capture clinical meaning unambiguously across healthcare systems.

#### Enduring Strategic Role
Harmonia requires authoritative clinical vocabularies to govern semantic meaning during data ingestion, correlation, and egress:
- **SNOMED CT-AU**: The Australian extension to SNOMED CT, providing the clinical concept reference hierarchy for clinical findings, diagnoses, procedures, and body structures.
- **Australian Medicines Terminology (AMT)**: The national standard terminology for medicinal products, supporting unambiguous medication reconciliation, prescribing concepts, and dispensing data.

These terminology assets enable Harmonia to evaluate clinical semantic equivalence, enforce conformance rules, and execute meaning-preserving transformations without corrupting clinical intent.

*(Note on LOINC: While Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes (LOINC) is a recognised international standard for laboratory and diagnostic observations, its formal admission as an enduring foundational resource is governed by jurisdictional adoption and is not pre-empted ahead of explicit canonical scope).*

#### Traceability
- **Architectural Axioms**: `AX-04` (Own Semantics / Use Machinery), `AX-08` (Meaning over Machinery), `AX-14` (Preserve Distinctions).
- **Enterprise Capabilities**: `EC-13` (Semantic Governance & Conformance).
- **Courses of Action**: `COA-03` (Meaning-Centric Provenance and Traceability).

## 3. Formal Relegation Dispositions

The following candidate assets were evaluated against the Strategic Significance Test and determined to belong outside Domain 02 Strategic Resources:

### Candidate 1: Healthcare Regulatory & Privacy Compliance Frameworks
- **Candidate Frameworks**: Privacy Act 1988, Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), My Health Record Act 2012, and state/territory health records legislation.
- **Strategic Significance Assessment**: These legal frameworks dictate mandatory compliance boundaries, consent models, and data sovereignty rules across Harmonia. However, they represent legal and societal constraints under which all platform capabilities must operate, rather than operational or architectural assets. Classifying them as Strategy Resources would duplicate the external constraints model without adding distinct architectural meaning. (Note: Foreign regulations such as HIPAA or GDPR are excluded as outside Harmonia's canonical Australian healthcare scope).
- **Disposition**: **CLASSIFY as External Constraints in Domain 01 Motivation** (`CON-01` through `CON-11`).

### Candidate 2: Authoritative Healthcare Provider Graph
- **Candidate Description**: The consolidated, reconciled graph of practitioners, organisations, healthcare services, and endpoints managed by Harmonia.
- **Strategic Significance Assessment**: The Provider Graph is an informational representation constructed and maintained *by* Harmonia through the ingestion and reconciliation of source system data and national directory feeds. It is managed health information, not an external or enabling strategic asset.
- **Disposition**: **RELEGATE to Domain 04 Information Architecture** (Managed Entity & Relationship `EC-01` and Information Management `EC-04`).

### Candidate 3: Vendor-Neutral Longitudinal Clinical Record
- **Candidate Description**: The longitudinal clinical history of patients aggregated across care settings.
- **Strategic Significance Assessment**: The longitudinal clinical record is the core clinical payload governed and preserved by Harmonia. It is the primary subject of Harmonia's information management capabilities, not an enabling strategic asset.
- **Disposition**: **RELEGATE to Domain 04 Information Architecture** (Information Management `EC-04`).

### Candidate 4: Canonical Pragma Task Envelope & Schema Library
- **Candidate Description**: The internal distributed unit-of-work envelope, task schema definitions, and execution contracts.
- **Strategic Significance Assessment**: The Pragma task envelope is an internal software architecture pattern used for distributed execution coordination. It belongs to application domain modeling rather than enterprise strategy.
- **Disposition**: **RELEGATE to Domain 05 Application Architecture / Calliope** (`EC-10`).

### Candidate 5: Kleio Audit & Provenance Evidence Trail
- **Candidate Description**: The durable, non-PHI security audit records, provenance assertions, and evidence trails generated during execution.
- **Strategic Significance Assessment**: Audit trails and provenance logs are operational compliance outputs generated through capability execution, not foundational strategic assets enabling the platform.
- **Disposition**: **RELEGATE to Domain 08 Security Architecture & Domain 04 Information Architecture** (Policy & Control `EC-06`, Provenance & Traceability `EC-07`).

### Candidate 6: Paradeigma Synthetic Persona & Simulation Testbeds
- **Candidate Description**: Synthetic clinical personas, simulated EMR/LIS systems, and test generation harnesses.
- **Strategic Significance Assessment**: Paradeigma provides valuable test harnesses and scenario simulations for offline verification. However, simulation testbeds are offline testing assets rather than production operational strategic resources. (Enforced by Invariant 1: Paradeigma Production Isolation).
- **Disposition**: **RELEGATE to Domain 10 Testing & Verification Architecture**.

## 4. Architectural Summary

Harmonia's Strategic Resources are intentionally kept minimal and enduring:
- **SR-01 (Healthcare Interoperability Standards and Specifications)** defines *how interoperability is achieved standardly*.
- **SR-02 (Australian National Healthcare Directory & Identifier Specifications)** defines *how healthcare participants and endpoints are authoritatively discovered and identified*.
- **SR-03 (National Clinical Terminology Assets)** defines *how clinical concepts are unambiguously understood*.

By restricting Strategic Resources to these foundational assets and relegating managed data, application envelopes, and verification tools to their appropriate architectural domains, Harmonia maintains clean separation between enterprise strategy, information modeling, and software realization.
